# Verification: Audit 2026-09-23

Checks of the audit's rule-based findings against primary sources, done before any code changes. Each finding is fixed from the result here, not from the audit's wording when the two differ.

- **Verdicts:** Confirmed (fix as the audit proposes), Corrected (the rule differs from the audit; fix to the rule here), Open (still needs a source).
- **Contract text:** Florida Realtors/Florida Bar AS IS Residential Contract for Sale and Purchase, ASIS-7x Rev. 2/26, and its riders (Condominium Rider CR-7x Rev. 05/2026; the others CR-7). Checked 2026-09-24. Paragraph numbers are the form's.

## FR/BAR Contract Rules

| ID | Verdict | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| TL-1 | Confirmed, with an addition | Time periods are counted in calendar days, based on where the Property is located. Any period that ends, or date that falls, on a Saturday, Sunday, national legal public holiday (5 U.S.C. 6103(a)) or observed holiday extends to the next calendar day that is none of those. There is no rule for periods of 5 days or less. Acceptance and the Effective Date (Para. 3) are excluded from the extension. **The form sets no time of day anywhere** (no 5:00 PM, no 11:59 PM), so a period runs to the end of its last day, and an extended one to the end of the next business day; the 5:00 PM rollover time came from older forms. Dates counted back from closing extend forward too, not earlier. | Standard F |
| TL-2 | Confirmed | Title Evidence Deadline: at least 15 days before the Closing Date if blank, or 5 days if Para. 8(a) (cash) is checked. | Para. 9(c) |
| TL-3 | Corrected | The FHA/VA rider has no appraisal deadline. The buyer isn't obligated to close if the appraised value is below the stated price. It has three receipt-based windows: the buyer's election to proceed regardless of the appraisal, within 3 days after receiving it (Para. 5); the seller's election on appraisal repairs above the cap, within 3 days after notice of the excess cost; and the buyer's election to pay the rest or cancel, within 3 days after the seller's election (3(b), 4(b)). Show these as standing notes, not dated rows. | CR-7 E |
| TL-6 | Confirmed | "Dates specified" extend too, so the Closing Date rolls forward to the next business day. | Standard F |
| TL-10 | Confirmed | The Effective Date is when the last of the buyer and seller "has signed or initialed and delivered" the offer or final counter-offer. | Para. 3(b) |
| TL-11 | Corrected | **Condo (nondeveloper):** the buyer may cancel within **7 days, excluding Saturdays, Sundays and legal holidays**, after the later of the buyer signing and the buyer receiving the declaration, articles, bylaws, rules, the latest financial statement and budget, and the FAQ document. The right can't be waived and ends at closing. The buyer may extend closing by up to 7 business days after receiving the documents. **Condo milestone inspection summary and SIRS:** a separate voidability right of 7 business days, same shape (Rider Para. 9(d)). **Condo association approval:** the contract is contingent on approval no later than 5 days before closing (default), and the seller starts the application within 5 days after the Effective Date. **HOA (s. 720.401):** when the disclosure summary wasn't given before signing, the buyer may cancel within 3 calendar days after receiving it; the right ends at closing. Association approval defaults are the same as the condo rider's. All of these are **buyer** rights and belong in the contingencies. The audit's "3 days" condo figure is from before the 2024 law change. | CR-7x A Paras. 3, 5, 9; CR-7 B |
| TL-12 | Confirmed, with paragraph fixes | **Title examination:** 5 days after the buyer receives the Title Commitment, with a closing extension of up to 5 days when it arrives less than 5 days before closing (Standard A(ii)). **Seller's existing survey:** furnished within 5 days after the Effective Date (Para. 9(d)). **Flood elevation cancellation:** 20 days after the Effective Date (Para. 10(d), not 10(c)). **Seller's right to terminate:** within 3 days after the Loan Approval Period expires when the buyer sent no notice (Para. 8(b)(v), not 8(b)(iv)). | ASIS-7 |
| TL-13 | Corrected | The buyer's survey defect notice is due within 5 days after receiving the Survey, **and no later than Closing** (not 5 days before closing). The Survey itself is due at least 5 days before closing (Para. 9(d)). | Standard B, Para. 9(d) |
| TL-18 | Confirmed | Walk-through: on the day before the Closing Date, or on the Closing Date before closing, as the buyer chooses. | Para. 12(b) |
| TL-23 | Confirmed (form) | The Lead-Based Paint rider lets the buyer waive the risk assessment or inspection. A waived right gets no row. | CR-7 P |
| CORE-18 | Corrected | The seller's search cap ($200 if blank) exists only in option (iii), the Miami-Dade/Broward regional provision. Under it the buyer designates the closing agent and pays the owner's policy, and the seller pays the title search (up to the cap), the tax search and the municipal lien search. Under option (ii) (buyer pays, statewide) there is no cap and the buyer pays the Charges. | Para. 9(c) |
| OFR-14 | Confirmed, with an addition | Taxes, CDD and special-district assessments, association fees and other recurring items are prorated through the day before closing. Due allowance is made for the maximum allowable discount and exemptions. When the current year's millage isn't set, proration uses the current assessment and the prior year's millage. Either party may ask to readjust once the bill arrives. | Standard K |
| CMA-3 | Confirmed | Same rule as OFR-14. CDD assessments are included in the tax proration. | Standard K |

Not in the form: ASIS-7 Rev. 2/26 Para. 10 has no seller flood disclosure paragraph. The statutory disclosure (CMA-6) is a separate form; see below.

| CMA-6 | Confirmed, with dates | **Seller flood disclosure (s. 689.302):** a seller of residential real property gives the statutory form at or before the contract is signed. It asks whether the seller knows of flooding that damaged the property during their ownership, has filed a flood claim (including NFIP), or received flood assistance (including FEMA). The claims and federal assistance questions started Oct 1, 2024 (ch. 2024-215); the knowledge question and any-assistance wording started Oct 1, 2025 (ch. 2025-166). No exemptions in the text. **Citizens flood requirement (s. 627.351(6)(aa), not (6)(a)):** flood coverage is required for personal residential policies effective on or after Jan 1, 2024 at $600,000+ dwelling replacement cost, Jan 1, 2025 at $500,000+, Jan 1, 2026 at $400,000+, and Jan 1, 2027 for all others; Special Flood Hazard Areas since 2023. Policies without wind coverage and HO-6 condo unit policies are exempt. So "flood insurance isn't required" in zone X is wrong for a Citizens policy at $400,000+ in 2026 and for every Citizens policy from 2027. | leg.state.fl.us s. 689.302, s. 627.351; ch. 2024-215, 2025-166 |
| CORE-7 | Corrected | **Owner's title payer (custom, not law):** buyer pays in Miami-Dade, Broward, Sarasota and Collier. Lee and Charlotte are seller-pay counties (the audit suggested buyer). Add Collier; leave Lee and Charlotte; mark Monroe as ask. **MLS coverage:** Pinellas is Stellar (Suncoast Tampa Association of REALTORS, a Stellar shareholder). **Brevard is not Stellar** (Space Coast MLS). Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach are MIAMI MLS and BeachesMLS, which merged associations on May 12, 2026 (combined MLS name to be set). | Florida TitleFund county survey (secondary); stellarmls.com shareholders; spacecoastmls.com |

A proposed amendment (HJR 1F, on the Nov 2026 ballot) would change non-school exemptions from 2027; not in effect. | s. 196.031; Florida DOR CPI homestead table (Jan 2026) |
| OFR-11 | Confirmed | 2026 conforming limit (1 unit): $832,750 baseline, $1,249,125 high-cost ceiling; in Florida only Monroe is above the baseline ($990,150). 2026 FHA (1 unit): floor $541,287, ceiling $1,249,125 (ML 2025-23). | OFR-19, TL-15 | Corrected | **The current Texas resale contract is TREC 20-19**, mandatory from July 1, 2026 (it replaced 20-18). Title commitment (Para. 6B): within 20 days after the title company receives the contract, extended automatically up to 15 days or 3 days before closing, whichever is earlier. Appraisal termination: TREC 49-1 (still current; not for FHA or VA). 20-19 has no general time paragraph: the Para. 5A extension is the only weekend and holiday rule. | trec.texas.gov forms 20-19 and 49-1 |
