# FR/BAR Comprehensive Rider (CR-7)

Read this when an FR/BAR AS IS or Standard contract lists riders in Para. 19, or when choosing riders for an offer. Each rider below says which form allows it, every blank and its default, the deadlines and cancel rights it creates, how it moves money, what it changes in the contract, what to check, and what each skill does with it. The contract paragraphs are in `frbar-contract.md`; addenda and disclosures are in `frbar-addenda.md`. Riders control over the printed contract (Standard R), and a rider counts only when every party initialed it.

## Reading Riders

- **Identify each rider by its letter,** the checked box in Para. 19 and the rider's own heading. Record the letters in the data file's `riders` (the scripts also accept the printed names). A checked box with no rider attached, or an attached rider that isn't checked, is a contract issue.
- **Riders I, K and L are RESERVED on the AS IS form** (Para. 19 of ASIS-7x). They belong only with the Standard form. On an AS IS contract they are a drafting error: stop and ask which form and riders were signed.
- **K and L change the Standard form's inspection terms.** K deletes the repair limits and Paras. 11 and 12 and gives a walk-away inspection period (AS IS math). L gives a walk-away Right To Inspect Period and keeps the repair limits (both rules). Never attach both.
- **EE was renamed.** The current form (CR-7x Rev. 2/26) is the Qualifying Improvements rider; older copies and listings call it the PACE Disclosure rider. Treat both names as EE.
- **Blanks with no default are not defaults.** Where a block says "no default (ask)", ask the agent or record an assumption; never fill in a number from another rider or from habit.
- **Many riders are silent on some outcomes** (what happens after a missed date, whether the deposit is refunded). The blocks give the safe reading and say "ask"; pass that to the agent rather than deciding it.
- **Dates:** rider periods follow the contract's time rules (calendar days; a period ending on a weekend or national legal holiday runs to the next business day) unless the rider says otherwise. The condominium rescission windows count business days.

## Riders A to P

### A. Condominium Rider

**Form:** `CR-7_A`, CR-7x Rev. 05/2026. **Allowed On:** Both forms.

- **Purpose:** Adds the condominium terms to the sale: association approval, rights of first refusal, assessments and fines, the s. 718.503 nondeveloper disclosure and rescission right, document requests, and the milestone inspection, turnover inspection and structural integrity reserve study (SIRS) disclosures with their own rescission right.
- **Blanks and Defaults:**
  - Header: association name, management company, contact person, phone, email and association website. No default (ask). If the property is also in a master or other homeowners' association, Rider B must be completed too; if it is in more than one condominium association, a separate Rider A is needed for each.
  - Para. 1: association approval "is / is not" required. No default (ask). Approval deadline: 5 days before Closing if blank. Seller starts the approval process: 5 days after Effective Date if blank.
  - Para. 2(a): association "has / does not have" a right of first refusal (ROFR). No default (ask). Para. 2(b): members "have / do not have" a ROFR. No default (ask). Para. 2(c): parties sign ROFR documents: 5 days after Effective Date if blank.
  - Para. 3(a): regular assessment amount and period (monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, annually); recreation area rent amount and period. No default (ask).
  - Para. 3(c)(i): who pays special assessments levied as of Effective Date: Seller if blank. Para. 3(c)(ii): who pays those levied after Effective Date and before Closing Date: Seller if blank. Para. 3(c)(iii): who pays installments due after Closing Date when the association lets the buyer assume them: Buyer if blank. Para. 3(c)(iv): seller lists special assessments levied or discussed at a board meeting in the 12 months before Effective Date (purpose and amount). No default.
  - Para. 3(d): pending or anticipated litigation exceptions. Blank means none disclosed.
  - Para. 5: check one of (a) buyer received the documents more than 7 business days before signing, or (b) contract voidable. No default printed (see Red Flags).
  - Para. 6(b): checkboxes for documents the buyer requests (board minutes and agendas for 12 months, member meeting minutes and agendas for 12 months, association insurance declaration pages, the milestone summary, turnover report or SIRS if Para. 9(a)(iii), 9(b)(iii) or 9(c)(iii) is checked and the seller receives it before Closing, plus 3 open lines). None requested if unchecked.
  - Para. 7: parking space number(s), garage number, other areas. No default.
  - Para. 9(a) milestone inspection, 9(b) turnover inspection report, 9(c) SIRS: each "check only one" of (i) required and completed, (ii) not required, (iii) required but not completed. No default (ask).
  - Para. 9(d): if any of 9(a)(i), 9(b)(i) or 9(c)(i) is checked, check one of (i) buyer received the reports more than 7 business days before signing, or (ii) contract voidable. No default printed.
- **Deadlines:**
  - 5 days after Effective Date (Para. 1): Seller starts the association approval process and gives Buyer the current application; Buyer then applies promptly. If missed: seller default risk; the approval deadline does not move.
  - 5 days before Closing (Para. 1): Buyer's association approval must be in hand. If missed: the contract terminates automatically and the Deposit is refunded to Buyer.
  - 5 days after Effective Date (Para. 2(c)): Buyer and Seller sign and deliver ROFR documents. If missed: that party's default risk; the ROFR window itself is set by the Declaration, not the rider.
  - Within the time the Declaration allows (Para. 2(d)–(e)): association or members exercise the ROFR. If exercised: contract terminates, Deposit refunded to Buyer (unless the contract provides otherwise). If waived in writing or not timely exercised: contract proceeds to Closing.
  - Before Closing (Para. 4): Seller delivers the association's written notice of a vote to forgo sprinkler or handrail/guardrail retrofitting (s. 718.112(2)(n), F.S.), if there was one. If missed: seller default risk.
  - 7 days excluding Saturdays, Sundays and legal holidays after the later of Buyer signing and Buyer receiving the Para. 5 documents (Para. 5(b)): Buyer may cancel. If missed: right ends. Right ends at Closing in any case.
  - 7 days excluding Saturdays, Sundays and legal holidays after Buyer receives ALL documents requested in Para. 6(b): Buyer may terminate. If missed: right ends. Ends at Closing.
  - 7 days excluding Saturdays, Sundays and legal holidays after the later of Buyer signing and Buyer receiving the milestone summary, turnover report and SIRS (Para. 9(d)(ii)): Buyer may cancel. If missed: right ends. Ends at Closing.
  - Closing extension (Para. 5(b) and 9(d)(ii)): Buyer may extend Closing up to 7 days excluding Saturdays, Sundays and legal holidays after receiving the documents.
- **Cancel Rights:**
  - Association approval not obtained by the Para. 1 deadline: automatic termination, Deposit refunded to Buyer.
  - ROFR exercised: automatic termination, Deposit refunded to Buyer unless the contract says otherwise.
  - Para. 5(b) (s. 718.503, F.S.): Buyer, 7 business days after signing and receiving the governing documents, budget, financial statement and FAQ; any waiver is void; ends at Closing. The rider does not state the deposit outcome; the statute makes the contract voidable, so treat as a full refund (confirm with the agent).
  - Para. 6(b): Buyer, 7 business days after receiving all requested documents; ends at Closing. Deposit outcome not stated; treat as refund and confirm.
  - Para. 9(d)(ii): Buyer, 7 business days after signing and receiving the milestone summary, turnover report and SIRS; waiver void; ends at Closing. Deposit outcome not stated; treat as refund and confirm.
- **Money Effects:**
  - Seller: brings regular assessments and recreation rent current at Closing (Para. 3(a)); pays all association fines as of Closing Date and cures noticed violations (Para. 3(b)); pays special assessments levied before Closing in full unless Buyer is checked (Para. 3(c)(i)–(ii)); pays installments due on or before Closing Date and, if Seller is checked or the association won't let the buyer assume, the full assessment (Para. 3(c)(iii)); pays for the Para. 5 documents, the governance form and any Para. 6(b) documents (Para. 6).
  - Buyer: pays special assessment installments due after Closing if the association allows assumption and Buyer is checked or the blank is empty (Para. 3(c)(iii)); returns documents or reimburses their cost if the deal does not close (Para. 6(a)).
  - Association reserves and other assets or liabilities are not prorated (Para. 3(a)). Regular periodic fees still prorate under STANDARD K.
  - From the contract, not the rider: Seller pays association estoppel fees (Para. 9(a)); Buyer pays association application and transfer fees (Para. 9(b)). The rider has no estoppel paragraph of its own.
- **Changes to the Contract:** Adds an association approval contingency and ROFR terms; Para. 3(c) governs condominium special assessments (the contract's Para. 9(f) covers only public-body assessments); Para. 8 limits the Paras. 11 and 12 maintain, repair, replace and treat duties to the unit itself, not common or limited common elements, unless Seller is otherwise responsible (on the AS IS form this narrows only the Para. 11 maintenance duty); adds statutory rescission rights that can extend Closing. Riders control printed terms (STANDARD R).
- **Red Flags:**
  - Para. 1 or 2 checkboxes blank: approval and ROFR exposure unknown; ask.
  - Para. 1 approval deadline (default 5 days before Closing) against the association's actual processing time; a short closing can fail on approval alone, and failure terminates the deal automatically.
  - Para. 3(c)(iii) default is Buyer: when an assessment can be paid in installments and the association allows assumption, the default shifts post-closing installments to the buyer even though (i) and (ii) default to Seller. Check which boxes are marked.
  - Para. 3(c)(iv) list blank while the listing or minutes show a pending assessment.
  - Neither Para. 5 box checked: the statute still gives the rescission right, so use the safe reading that the contract is voidable under 5(b).
  - Para. 5(a) checked without proof of delivery more than 7 business days before signing: treat the right as open.
  - Para. 9(a), 9(b) or 9(c) left blank, or a (iii) box (required but not completed): a SIRS or milestone gap affects lending and future assessments.
  - Any 9(x)(i) checked but no 9(d) box checked: use the safe reading that 9(d)(ii) applies (voidable).
  - The rider sets no seller deadline to deliver the Para. 5, 6 or 9 documents; each rescission clock starts only on receipt, so the deal is not firm until they are delivered and the windows pass.
  - Para. 10 applies only to a condominium in part of a building or a multiple-parcel building (s. 718.407, F.S.); check the shared-cost allocation in the recorded instruments.
  - A master HOA with no Rider B attached.
- **Skill Use:**
  - Timeline: add approval start (ED + 5), ROFR paperwork (ED + 5), approval deadline (Closing − 5), and each rescission window as "7 business days after receipt" with the receipt date the agent supplies; show "pending receipt" when unknown; note all rights end at Closing and the buyer's 7-business-day closing extension.
  - Seller review: count unpaid special assessments and fines against net per the checked boxes; lower certainty until the 7-business-day windows close and association approval is in; flag a short closing with approval required.
  - Buyer builder: recommend checking Buyer or Seller deliberately in Para. 3(c); request the 6(b) documents (minutes, insurance pages, SIRS); warn that FHA or VA needs an approved project and that an incomplete milestone or SIRS can mean future assessments.

### B. Homeowners' Association/Community Disclosure Rider

**Form:** `CR-7_B`, CR-7 Rev. 10/21. **Allowed On:** Both forms.

- **Purpose:** Delivers the s. 720.401, F.S. disclosure summary for a mandatory homeowners' association and sets association approval, fee and assessment terms (Part B).
- **Blanks and Defaults:**
  - Part A: community name; regular assessment amount and period; special assessment amount and period; recreational or land use fee amount and period; buyer signature and date. No default (ask).
  - Part B, Para. 1: approval "is / is not" required. No default (ask). Approval deadline: 5 days before Closing if blank. Seller starts approval: 5 days after Effective Date if blank.
  - Part B, Para. 2(a): up to 4 lines of fees (amount, per, for, to whom). No default.
  - Part B, Para. 2(b): who pays installments due after Closing Date: Buyer if blank.
  - Association or management company contacts and website. No default.
- **Deadlines:**
  - 3 days after Buyer receives the disclosure summary, or before Closing, whichever is first (Part A): Buyer may cancel if the summary was not provided before signing. If missed: right ends. Calendar days under the contract's time rules.
  - 5 days after Effective Date (Part B, Para. 1): Seller starts the approval process. If missed: seller default risk.
  - 5 days before Closing (Part B, Para. 1): association approval due. If missed: Buyer may terminate and get the Deposit back (not automatic).
- **Cancel Rights:**
  - Part A (s. 720.401, F.S.): Buyer, only if the summary came after signing; within 3 days after receipt or before Closing, whichever is first; waiver void. Deposit outcome not stated; treat as a full refund and confirm.
  - Part B, Para. 1: Buyer, if approval is not granted by the deadline; Deposit refunded. The rider sets no cut-off for this election; the safe reading is that Buyer must act before Closing (ask the agent).
- **Money Effects:**
  - Buyer pays application and related fees unless the governing documents or the parties say otherwise (Para. 1), and application, initial contribution, membership or other association fees (Para. 2(a)).
  - Seller pays special or other assessments existing at Effective Date or levied before Closing that are due in full before Closing (Para. 2(b)); for installment assessments, Seller pays installments due before Closing Date and Buyer (default) or Seller pays those after; if Seller is checked, Seller pays the full assessment.
  - Seller pays all association fines existing at Closing and the fees the association charges to provide information about the property, assessments and fees (Para. 2(c)), which is where estoppel-type charges land. The contract's Para. 9(a) also puts association estoppel fees on Seller.
  - Regular association fees prorate under STANDARD K.
- **Changes to the Contract:** Satisfies the Para. 10(g) disclosure warning; adds an association approval contingency; sets association assessment allocation (Para. 9(f) excludes associations). CDD and special district assessments stay under STANDARD K, not this rider.
- **Red Flags:**
  - The rider's own header says the disclosure was given before signing. If Part A was signed or dated after the offer, the 3-day right is open until Closing or 3 days after receipt.
  - Approval box blank; approval deadline too close to Closing.
  - Para. 2(b) blank means Buyer pays post-closing installments.
  - Part A amounts blank or out of date against the listing.
  - A condominium sale using only Rider B (a condo needs Rider A).
- **Skill Use:**
  - Timeline: approval start (ED + 5), approval due (Closing − 5), and the Part A window (3 days after receipt, until Closing) only when the summary came after signing.
  - Seller review: count assessments and fines the seller owes; certainty drops if approval is required and slow.
  - Buyer builder: include the fee amounts in cash to close; decide Para. 2(b) deliberately; deliver the summary before the buyer signs so no rescission window is created.

### C. Seller Financing Rider

**Form:** `CR-7_C`, CR-7 Rev. 10/21. **Allowed On:** Both forms.

- **Purpose:** Sets the terms of a purchase money note and mortgage from Buyer to Seller (contract Para. 8(d)), within the Dodd-Frank seller-financing exclusions summarized in Section I.
- **Blanks and Defaults:**
  - Section I: explains the "one property" and "three property" exclusions (fixed rate, or adjustable with no change for 5 years and reasonable caps; no negative amortization; the three-property exclusion also bars balloons and requires a good-faith ability-to-repay determination). No blanks. A seller outside both exclusions should get legal advice before using the rider.
  - Section II: first or second mortgage (check one). No default (ask). Principal amount and interest rate. No default (ask).
  - Loan type, check one: (a) fully amortized, term 30 years if blank; (b) interest only, term 60 months if blank (one-property exclusion only); (c) balloon, amortized over 30 years if blank (one-property exclusion only), balloon due 60 months after Closing if blank; (d) adjustable, minimum term 30 years if blank. The form says no other options are permitted.
  - ARM (option (d)): first change after 5 years if blank (no fewer than 5), then every 1 year if blank; margin 1 percentage point if blank; periodic cap 2 points if blank (no more than 2); lifetime cap 6 points if blank (no more than 6); index: 1-year constant maturity Treasury weekly average, as of 45 days before each Change Date, if blank.
  - Payments: monthly, quarterly or annual (check one), payment amount, first payment months after Closing. No default (ask).
- **Deadlines:** None. The rider sets no deadline for credit review, loan documents or seller approval of the buyer.
- **Cancel Rights:** None. The rider gives neither party a right to cancel.
- **Money Effects:**
  - Seller receives the note instead of cash for the principal amount, so cash to Seller at Closing drops by that amount (net proceeds become part cash, part note).
  - Buyer's cash to close falls by the principal; Buyer must keep hazard and extended-coverage insurance with a standard mortgagee clause at highest insurable value.
  - Note terms fixed by the rider: 30-day grace period on a first mortgage, 15-day on a second or lower; prepayment in whole or part without penalty; acceleration on transfer (due on sale); prior liens kept current; no modification of or future advances under a prior mortgage; a default on the first is, at the holder's option, a default on the second.
  - If a second mortgage, the first mortgage may not exceed the contract's Para. 2(c) loan amount.
  - At Seller's option, personal property and leases conveyed are subject to a recorded security agreement.
- **Changes to the Contract:** Supplies the terms for Para. 8(d); ties a second-mortgage structure to Para. 2(c). Loan documents are in the form Seller requires, limited to clauses customary for banks and residential lenders in the county.
- **Red Flags:**
  - Principal, rate, payment, or first/second box blank: no default; the terms are incomplete.
  - Interest-only or balloon checked when Seller does not fit the one-property exclusion.
  - Payment amount that does not match principal, rate and term.
  - Option (d) text refers to the index "identified in (c)(iii), below," but the index is in (d)(iii); treat (d)(iii) as the intended reference.
  - No credit or approval contingency for Seller: the seller has no contractual way to review or reject the buyer's ability to pay.
  - If Para. 8(b) third-party financing is also checked for a first mortgage, confirm the lender allows seller seconds.
- **Skill Use:**
  - Timeline: no rider deadlines; note the balloon due date (Closing + balloon months) and first payment date as post-closing items.
  - Seller review: count the note principal as deferred proceeds, not cash; lower certainty of collection; flag the missing credit check and Dodd-Frank eligibility.
  - Buyer builder: show reduced cash to close; warn about the balloon date, due-on-sale clause and insurance requirement; recommend legal review.

### D. Assumption of Existing Mortgage Rider

**Form:** `CR-7_D`, CR-7 Rev. 10/21. **Allowed On:** Both forms.

- **Purpose:** Sets terms for Buyer assuming Seller's existing mortgage (contract Para. 8(c)).
- **Blanks and Defaults:**
  - Existing mortgage amount. No default (ask).
  - Variable rate, or fixed rate %. No default (ask).
  - Maximum rate if the fixed rate increases at transfer. No default (ask).
  - Mortgagee charge cap paid by Buyer: 1% of the amount assumed if blank.
- **Deadlines:**
  - No day count. Seller furnishes a statement from each mortgagee (balance, payment method, rate, status) or authorizes Buyer or Closing Agent to get it. Buyer "promptly" obtains and diligently completes the assumption application. If missed: default risk for the late party; no stated cure period.
- **Cancel Rights:** Seller or Buyer may rescind by written notice if the mortgagee does not accept Buyer, the assumption terms differ from the contract, or the mortgagee's charge exceeds the cap, unless either party elects to pay the rate increase or excess charges. The rider sets no time limit and does not state the deposit outcome; safe reading is a refund to Buyer when Buyer is not in default (confirm with the agent).
- **Money Effects:** Buyer pays mortgagee charges up to the cap. Either party may choose to pay the excess or rate increase to keep the deal. The assumed balance replaces part of the buyer's cash. Under STANDARD K, escrow deposits held by Seller's mortgagee are paid to Seller.
- **Changes to the Contract:** Supplies the terms for Para. 8(c).
- **Red Flags:** Amount or rate blank; no deadline for mortgagee approval (ask the agent to set one); the rider does not address a release of Seller's liability on the assumed loan; confirm the loan is actually assumable.
- **Skill Use:**

### E. FHA/VA Rider

**Form:** `CR-7_E`, CR-7 Rev. 10/21. **Allowed On:** Both forms.

- **Purpose:** Adds the FHA or VA amendatory (escape) clause, a seller cap for lender-required appraisal repairs, and FHA/VA fee allocations.
- **Blanks and Defaults:**
  - Para. 1: definitions (Contract, Property, HUD, VA, Purchaser = Buyer). No blanks.
  - Para. 2: Seller's cap for Appraisal Repairs (lender-required inspections and appraisal repairs), $. No default (ask). This cap is in addition to other costs the contract makes Seller pay.
  - Para. 3: FHA box (check if applicable). Minimum appraised value, $. No default (ask; normally the purchase price). Para. 3(a): tax service fee Seller pays: $100 if blank.
  - Para. 4: VA box (check if applicable). No value blank (the test is the VA reasonable value against the price). Para. 4(a): VA fees Seller pays: $250 if blank.
  - Signature lines for Buyer, Seller and broker/sales associate with dates (the Para. 3(c) certification).
- **Deadlines:**
  - 3 days after Seller receives notice that Appraisal Repairs exceed the Para. 2 cap (Para. 3(b) or 4(b)): Seller gives written notice of paying all, some or none of the excess. If missed: the rider does not say; ask the agent.
  - 3 days after Buyer receives Seller's election (Para. 3(b) or 4(b)): if Seller pays less than all, Buyer elects in writing to pay the balance or cancel. If missed: the rider does not say; the safe reading is that the buyer's cancel right lapses (ask the agent).
  - 3 days after Purchaser receives the appraisal (Para. 5): Buyer's election to proceed despite a low appraised value must be made. If missed: the rider does not say; the escape clause protection is not otherwise time-limited, so treat it as running to Closing.
- **Cancel Rights:**
  - FHA (Para. 3) and VA (Para. 4) amendatory clause: Buyer is not obligated to close and forfeits no deposit if the FHA appraised value is below the stated amount, or the VA reasonable value is below the price. Buyer may choose to proceed anyway. No deadline to use the escape beyond the Para. 5 election to proceed.
  - Appraisal Repairs over cap: Buyer may cancel if Seller will not pay the full excess and Buyer won't pay the balance. Deposit outcome not stated; treat as refund and confirm.
- **Money Effects:** Seller pays Appraisal Repairs up to the Para. 2 cap, plus any excess Seller elects; FHA: Buyer pays all loan expenses except the tax service fee, which Seller pays up to $100 default; VA: Seller pays required VA fees up to $250 default, Buyer pays prepayments and escrows for taxes, hazard and flood insurance. A low appraisal can force a price reduction, which lowers Seller's net; per Para. 5 a price change needs no new rider, but the loan file must include the original and amended contracts.
- **Changes to the Contract:** Overrides the deposit and default terms of Paras. 8 and 15 to the extent of the escape clause ("notwithstanding any other provisions"). On the Standard form, the Appraisal Repairs cap is in addition to the Para. 9(a) repair limits. On the AS IS form, Para. 2 creates a seller repair obligation for lender-required items up to the cap, overriding Para. 12(a)'s statement that Buyer pays lender-required repairs (riders control, STANDARD R).
- **Red Flags:**
  - Para. 2 cap blank: no default. Ambiguous whether Seller owes $0 or an open amount; ask.
  - FHA value blank, or neither the FHA nor the VA box checked.
  - AS IS form with this rider: the seller has a repair cost exposure the seller may not expect.
  - The escape clause survives appraisal gap or waiver language elsewhere unless the buyer elects to proceed in writing; an appraisal gap offer on FHA/VA needs that written election.
  - Certification signatures missing (the lender will need them).
- **Skill Use:**
  - Timeline: add "appraisal received" as an open event with the 3-day election to proceed, and the 3 + 3 day repair election chain when a repair overage is noticed; note the escape protection runs to Closing.
  - Seller review: count the Para. 2 cap and default fees ($100 FHA or $250 VA unless filled) as seller costs; lower certainty for the appraisal escape; model a low-appraisal price cut.
  - Buyer builder: fill the Para. 2 cap deliberately; warn that the seller may not know about repair exposure on an AS IS form; an appraisal gap commitment requires the buyer to waive the escape in writing.

**Form:** `CR-7_F`, CR-7 Rev. 10/21. **Allowed On:** Both forms.

### G. Short Sale Approval Contingency Rider

**Form:** `CR-7_G`, CR-7 Rev. 10/21. **Allowed On:** Both forms.

- **Purpose:** Makes the sale contingent on the seller's lender(s) and lien holders approving a short payoff, and moves most contract clocks to start on the buyer's receipt of that approval.
- **Blanks and Defaults:**
  - Para. 2: days after Effective Date for Seller to get the short sale application: 10 if blank.
  - Para. 4: Short Sale Approval Deadline: 90 days from Effective Date if blank.
  - Para. 6: Closing Date: 45 days after Buyer receives Short Sale Approval if blank.
  - Para. 7: back-up offers, check one of (a) Seller may not accept back-ups, (b) Seller may accept back-up contracts conditioned on this deal failing. Option (a) if neither is checked.
- **Deadlines:**
  - 3 days after Seller receives a Short Sale Approval that Seller accepts (Para. 1): Seller delivers a copy to Buyer and Closing Agent. If missed: seller default risk.
  - 10 days after Effective Date (Para. 2): Seller gets the lender's short sale application forms. If missed: seller default risk.
  - 5 days after getting the forms (Para. 2): Seller completes and returns them, then promptly sends any added documents. If missed: seller default risk.
  - 90 days after Effective Date, the Short Sale Approval Deadline (Para. 4): if Seller has not delivered accepted approval, either party may terminate by written notice. If missed: either party may terminate any time afterward; Deposit refunded.
  - 30 days after the Short Sale Approval Deadline, including any extension, the Contract Expiration Date (Para. 4): contract terminates automatically if approval is not delivered. Deposit refunded.
  - Initial deposit (contract Para. 2(a)) still runs from Effective Date (Para. 5).
  - All other contract periods (additional deposit, inspection, loan approval, title, and so on) run from Buyer's receipt of Short Sale Approval (Para. 5).
  - 45 days after Buyer receives Short Sale Approval (Para. 6): Closing Date.
- **Cancel Rights:**
  - Either party, after the Short Sale Approval Deadline, by written notice; Deposit refunded to Buyer.
  - Automatic termination at the Contract Expiration Date; Deposit refunded.
  - Automatic termination if the property is sold at foreclosure before approval and Closing (Para. 10); Deposit refunded.
- **Money Effects:**
  - An approval that does not waive and fully release the lender's deficiency claim against Seller, or that adds terms or obligations for either party, is not Short Sale Approval unless the affected party accepts it in writing (Para. 1). Any lender-required changes (for example a price increase or seller contribution) need that party's written acceptance.
  - Seller's net is set by the lender; the seller typically receives no proceeds (confirm from the approval letter).
  - Buyer bears its own costs (loan application, inspections, appraisal) if the lender does not complete the short sale after approval; Seller and Broker are not liable for lender delays (Para. 9(b)).
- **Changes to the Contract:** Replaces the Para. 4 Closing Date with the Para. 6 formula; shifts all time periods except the initial deposit and the approval deadline to start at Buyer's receipt of approval; adds termination on foreclosure sale; restricts back-up offers by default. Para. 1 refers to the "HUD-1 settlement statement," an older name; read it as the settlement statement or Closing Disclosure used at Closing.
- **Red Flags:**
  - The rider has no built-in extension mechanism; Para. 4 mentions "any extension(s)," so an extension must be a signed amendment. Recompute the Contract Expiration Date after any extension.
  - An approval letter with deficiency language, a shorter closing window, or new costs is not approval until the affected party accepts in writing.
  - A fixed Para. 4 Closing Date conflicts with Para. 6; riders control (STANDARD R), so use Para. 6 and ask the agent.
  - Inspection period and loan approval do not start until approval: a buyer's rate lock or loan commitment may expire first.
  - Back-up box unchecked means Seller may not accept back-ups.
- **Skill Use:**
  - Timeline: show two phases. Phase 1 from Effective Date: initial deposit, application (ED + 10, then + 5), approval deadline (ED + 90 default), expiration (deadline + 30). Phase 2 from "approval received" (a date the agent enters): every other contract deadline and Closing (+ 45 default). Show Phase 2 as pending until that date is known. Rider GG's compensation agreement window stays in Phase 1, counted from the Effective Date (the earlier, safe reading); note that Para. 5's "all other time periods" could be read to move it. A closing date written in Para. 4 doesn't set Closing: Para. 6 does (ask the agent when both are filled in). The 30-day expiration counts from the approval deadline as it stands (after any weekend or holiday roll, and any extension). Once approval is received, the approval deadline is met and the Contract Expiration row no longer applies. Rider G Para. 2's second step (Seller returns the completed forms 5 days after getting them) is its own row, and so is Para. 1's copy of the accepted approval to Buyer and Closing Agent (3 days after Seller receives it, pending until that date is known). Tell the agent which Para. 7 back-up option applies ((a) when neither box is checked).
  - Seller review: very low certainty until approval; net governed by the lender; note back-up restriction.
  - Buyer builder: warn about long, open timelines and sunk costs; recommend a realistic approval deadline and holding inspections until approval.

- **Blanks and Defaults:** Party names and property description (header). One checkbox for the provider's stated designation: "62 years of age and older" or "55 years of age and older"; no default (ask). All parties initial.
- **Deadlines:** None.
- **Cancel Rights:** None. The rider gives neither party a right to cancel.
- **Money Effects:** None.
- **Changes to the Contract:** Adds an acknowledgment by Buyer; changes no paragraph. Seller and Broker expressly make no representation that the Property actually qualifies for the exemption; the statement is the housing provider's. The rider sets no eligibility condition in the contract. Any occupancy or approval rules come from the community's governing documents and the provider's own process, which run through the association approval terms (Condominium Rider A or HOA Rider B) when attached.
- **Red Flags:** Neither box or both boxes checked. Designation on the rider differs from the association documents or the listing. No association approval terms attached when the community requires approval. Any added language that describes who the property suits or who may live there beyond the stated legal designation (fair housing: rewrite as the designation only).
- **Skill Use:** Timeline: no row; if the community requires approval, the association approval dates come from Rider A or B. Seller offer review: no effect on net; treat an unresolved association approval as a certainty item only when a condo/HOA approval rider is attached. Buyer offer builder: when the listing shows the designation, attach Rider Q and tell the agent to read the governing documents for occupancy and approval rules; state the designation only, never describe who the home suits.

### R. Rezoning Contingency Rider

**Form:** `CR-7_R`, CR-7 Rev. 10/21. **Allowed On:** Both forms.

- **Purpose:** Makes Buyer's obligations contingent on Buyer obtaining, at Buyer's expense, a rezoning or land use change to a stated category (plus any Comprehensive Land Use Plan amendment needed), with a drop-dead date and a closing timed off the final approval.
- **Blanks and Defaults:** Target zoning or land use category: no default (ask). Jurisdiction checkbox, county or city: no default (ask). "Date" for final government action: no default (ask). Maximum extension after Date when the final public hearing is already scheduled: 10 days if left blank. Days to close after final rezoning approval (and land use change, if any): 10 days if left blank.
- **Deadlines:**
  - Promptly after Effective Date (no number of days): Buyer applies for and diligently pursues the rezoning and any CLUP amendment. If missed: not a stated deadline; lack of diligence is a Buyer default risk (Para. 15(a)).
  - On the Date: final government action on the rezoning application is due. If missed: either party may terminate by written notice after the Date; Deposit refunded to Buyer.
  - Extension: if on the Date the final public hearing is already scheduled before the board with final authority, the deadline moves to the day after that hearing, capped at the extension days (default 10) after the Date. If missed: same termination right for either party.
  - Closing days (default 10) after final rezoning approval and, if applicable, land use change: Closing occurs. If missed: ordinary default rules (Para. 15).
- **Cancel Rights:** Either Buyer or Seller, by written notice any time after the Date (or the extended deadline) while final government action has not been obtained. The rider sets no end to this window. Deposit refunded to Buyer.
- **Money Effects:** Buyer pays all application and pursuit costs. No credits or escrows. Seller's net is unchanged but delayed; the whole Deposit is refundable until approval.
- **Changes to the Contract:** Adds a contingency to Buyer's obligations. Replaces the fixed Closing Date in Para. 4 with "N days after final approval". Seller agrees to the rezoning and any CLUP amendment (consent as owner). Standard A title exceptions for zoning and land use still apply.
- **Red Flags:** Blank Date or blank target category. County vs. city not checked. Date too short for the local hearing calendar. Rider says "final government action", not "approval": if the application is denied before the Date the rider does not say either party may terminate at once; safe reading is that the condition has failed but the written termination right opens only after the Date (ask the agent or an attorney). Para. 4 Closing Date filled with a fixed date that conflicts with the rider.
- **Skill Use:** Timeline: add the Date, the capped extension date (Date + extension days), and "Closing = approval date + N days" as a floating row; note the open-ended termination window after the Date. Seller offer review: count the Deposit as fully refundable until approval, closing date as unknown, and certainty as low (government approval outside both parties' control). Buyer offer builder: warn the buyer pays all rezoning costs and must pursue it diligently; recommend a Date that fits the jurisdiction's hearing schedule.

### S. Lease Purchase/Lease Option Rider

**Form:** `CR-7_S`, CR-7 Rev. 10/21. **Allowed On:** Both forms.

- **Purpose:** Makes the Contract contingent on the parties signing a separate lease/purchase or lease/option agreement on mutually agreeable terms within 5 days after Effective Date. All lease, rent, option and credit terms live in that separate agreement, not in the rider.
- **Blanks and Defaults:** Agreement type (lease/purchase or lease/option): the extracted form shows both labels; confirm which is checked, no default (ask). Who pays the attorney's fees to prepare the agreement, check one of Buyer, Seller, or split equally: split equally if not checked. The 5-day period is preprinted, not a blank.
- **Deadlines:** 5 days after Effective Date: Buyer and Seller execute the lease/purchase or lease/option agreement. If missed: the Contract terminates automatically and the Deposit is refunded to Buyer.
- **Cancel Rights:** No notice needed: termination is automatic if the agreement is not executed in 5 days. Either party can in effect cause this by not agreeing to terms. Deposit refunded to Buyer.
- **Money Effects:** Attorney's fees for drafting the agreement (per the checkbox; default split equally). Rent, option fee, rent credits toward price and who holds them are not in the rider; read them from the separate agreement.
- **Changes to the Contract:** Adds a 5-day contingency. In practice the separate agreement governs possession, rent and timing; the rider does not say how Para. 4 Closing Date, Para. 6 occupancy or Para. 8 financing periods fit the lease term. Safe reading: treat the executed lease agreement as controlling those items and ask the agent or attorney where it conflicts with the Contract.
- **Red Flags:** Separate agreement not signed by day 5. Closing Date in Para. 4 that falls before the lease term ends. Loan Approval Period in Para. 8(b) running from an Effective Date months before the intended purchase. Option fee or rent credits described only verbally.
- **Skill Use:** Timeline: add "Day 5: lease/purchase or lease/option agreement signed or Contract terminates"; then take dates from the executed lease agreement. Seller offer review: treat price and net as provisional until the agreement is signed; certainty low until day 5 and dependent on the lease terms. Buyer offer builder: warn the whole deal dies automatically at day 5 without a signed agreement; recommend having the lease agreement drafted before or with the offer.

### T. Pre-Closing Occupancy by Buyer Rider

**Form:** `CR-7_T`, CR-7 Rev. 10/21. **Allowed On:** Both forms (see the AS IS note under Red Flags).

- **Purpose:** Lets Buyer move in before Closing under a separate Pre-Closing Agreement (lease, occupancy agreement or similar) and sets the minimum terms that agreement must contain: condition acceptance, maintenance, risk of loss and monthly rent.
- **Blanks and Defaults:** Days after Effective Date to deliver the Pre-Closing Agreement: 10 if left blank. Who pays to prepare it, check one of Seller, Buyer, or split equally: split equally if not checked. Possession date: no default (ask). Monthly rent: no default (ask); "plus applicable sales tax, if any", payable monthly in advance.
- **Deadlines:**
  - N days (default 10) after Effective Date: Buyer and Seller deliver to each other a mutually agreeable written Pre-Closing Agreement. If missed and Buyer has not taken occupancy: either party may terminate by written notice; Deposit refunded to Buyer.
  - Possession date (as filled in): Buyer takes possession; from then Buyer pays rent monthly in advance, maintains the Property and bears risk of loss. If missed: the rider states no consequence; the Pre-Closing Agreement governs.
- **Cancel Rights:** Either party, by written notice, only if no Pre-Closing Agreement was agreed within the period and Buyer has not yet taken occupancy. The rider sets no deadline for sending this notice; once Buyer takes occupancy the right is gone. Deposit refunded to Buyer.
- **Money Effects:** Monthly rent plus any sales tax paid by Buyer to Seller in advance (adds to Seller's proceeds before Closing; adds to Buyer's cash outlay). Agreement preparation cost per the checkbox. Standard K prorates taxes, association fees, insurance and other expenses as of the occupancy date, not the day before Closing, so Buyer carries those costs from move-in. Buyer carries risk of loss, so the Standard M casualty repair and 1.5% escrow protection for Buyer no longer applies after occupancy. Seller's repair obligations and any Para. 9(a) repair escrow (Standard form) end on possession except for items Buyer already reported under Para. 12.
- **Changes to the Contract:** Overrides Para. 6(a) delivery of possession at Closing (Para. 6(a) itself cross-refers to Rider T). On possession, Para. 11 (Property Maintenance) and Para. 12 no longer apply, and Buyer accepts the Property in its existing condition, except items Buyer identified under Para. 12 before taking occupancy that need repair, replacement, treatment or remedy. Buyer takes over maintenance and all risk of loss (overrides Standard M). Proration date moves to the occupancy date (Standard K).
- **Red Flags:** Possession date or rent blank. Possession set before the Inspection Period ends (Buyer loses the Para. 11 and 12 protections early). On the AS IS form, Para. 12 is the inspection and right to cancel, not repairs: the rider says Para. 12 "no longer applicable" on possession, so moving in before the Inspection Period ends may end Buyer's AS IS cancel right; safe reading: schedule possession after the Inspection Period and ask the agent or attorney. No insurance terms. No terms for what happens if the deal does not close after Buyer moves in (move-out date, holdover rent, damage, Deposit): the rider is silent; these must be in the Pre-Closing Agreement. Loan or lender restrictions on pre-closing occupancy not checked.
- **Skill Use:** Timeline: add "Day N: Pre-Closing Agreement delivered" (default 10), the possession date, a monthly rent-due row from the possession date, and a note that prorations run from the occupancy date. Seller offer review: count pre-closing rent as income to Seller, drop the Seller's post-occupancy repair and casualty exposure, and flag the risk of an occupant Buyer if the loan fails (certainty down unless the agreement has strong exit terms). Buyer offer builder: warn that moving in ends repair rights and shifts all risk of loss and upkeep to the buyer; recommend possession only after inspection and loan approval, with renter's or owner's coverage in place.

### U. Post-Closing Occupancy by Seller Rider

**Form:** `CR-7_U`, CR-7 Rev. 10/21. **Allowed On:** Both forms.

- **Purpose:** Lets Seller stay in the Property for a set number of days after Closing under a separate Post-Closing Agreement, paying monthly rent, with Seller's maintenance duty continuing until possession is delivered.
- **Blanks and Defaults:** Days before Closing Date to deliver the Post-Closing Agreement: 10 if left blank. Who pays to prepare it, check one of Seller, Buyer, or split equally: split equally if not checked. Days after Closing that Seller may stay: no default (ask). Monthly rent: no default (ask), payable monthly in advance.
- **Deadlines:**
  - N days (default 10) before Closing Date: Buyer and Seller deliver to each other a mutually acceptable Post-Closing Agreement. If missed: either party may terminate by written notice; Deposit refunded to Buyer.
  - N days after Closing (as filled in): Seller delivers possession to Buyer. If missed: the rider states no remedy; holdover terms must come from the Post-Closing Agreement.
- **Cancel Rights:** Either party, by written notice, if the Post-Closing Agreement is not delivered by the deadline. The rider sets no end date for sending the notice. Deposit refunded to Buyer.
- **Money Effects:** Seller pays Buyer monthly rent in advance (reduces Seller's net; offsets Buyer's carrying cost). Agreement preparation cost per the checkbox. The rider creates no escrow, holdback or security deposit; any holdback from Seller's proceeds for damage or late move-out must be written into the Post-Closing Agreement. Seller's Para. 12 repair obligations (Standard form) end at Closing; Seller's Para. 11 maintenance duty continues until possession.
- **Changes to the Contract:** Overrides Para. 6(a) delivery of possession, keys and removal of personal items at Closing (Para. 6(b) itself cross-refers to Rider U). Para. 6(b) is usually checked for the Seller's post-closing occupancy; that is not a tenancy: record no tenants (no estoppel or lease rows) unless a separate lease exists. Extends Para. 11 maintenance past Closing until possession is delivered. Confirms Para. 12 repair, replacement, treatment and remedy obligations do not extend past Closing. Walk-through (Standard L, Para. 12) happens before Seller moves out, so condition at move-out is not covered by the Contract.
- **Red Flags:** Days after Closing or rent blank. No holdback or security deposit for damage or holdover. No holdover rent or daily penalty. Risk of loss after Closing is not addressed in the rider: Buyer owns the Property while Seller occupies it; confirm insurance (Buyer's owner policy and Seller's renter's coverage) in the agreement. Lender occupancy rules: an owner-occupied loan may limit how long Seller can stay; ask Buyer's lender. The deadline is keyed to Closing Date; if Closing moves, confirm whether the deadline moves with it (ask the agent).
- **Skill Use:** Timeline: add "Closing Date minus N days (default 10): Post-Closing Agreement delivered", a Seller move-out row at Closing + N days, and a note that the final walk-through does not cover move-out condition. When Para. 6(b) is checked only for this stay, its 5-day lease disclosure and cancel windows aren't dated; tell the agent why. Seller offer review: count the rent-back rent as a cost to Seller (days x monthly rent / 30 as an estimate, labeled "Estimate") and any holdback as delayed proceeds; a rent-back offered by Buyer is a strong non-price term. Buyer offer builder: offering a rent-back can win a deal; recommend a holdback from Seller's proceeds, a holdover charge, a clear move-out date within the lender's occupancy rules, and insurance terms in the agreement.

### V. Sale of Buyer's Property Rider

**Form:** `CR-7_V`, CR-7 Rev. 10/21. **Allowed On:** Both forms.

- **Purpose:** Makes the Contract contingent on the sale and closing of Buyer's other property by a set date, with a short window after that date for Buyer to cancel.
- **Blanks and Defaults:** Address of Buyer's property: no default (ask). Date by which Buyer's sale must close: no default (ask); it is a calendar date, not a number of days. Checkbox, Buyer's property "is" or "is not" presently under contract: no default (ask). The 3-day notice window and the 24-hour delivery window are preprinted.
- **Deadlines:**
  - With the offer, or no later than 24 hours (hours, not days) after Buyer's offer is delivered to Seller, if Buyer's property is already under contract: Buyer gives Seller a copy of that contract with the third parties' identities and price blacked out. If missed: the rider states no remedy; treat as a Buyer breach and ask the agent.
  - When Buyer later signs a contract to sell Buyer's property (no number of days; "upon entering"): Buyer gives Seller a redacted copy. If missed: no stated remedy; ask.
  - The filled-in date: Buyer's sale must close. If it has not closed, go to the next row.
  - 3 days after that date: Buyer may deliver written notice terminating the Contract; Deposit refunded to Buyer. If missed: the sale contingency ends ("no further force or effect"); Buyer must close without the sale, and the Deposit is then at risk under Para. 15(a).
- **Cancel Rights:** Buyer only, by written notice within 3 days after the filled-in date, and only if Buyer's sale has not closed by that date. Deposit refunded. Seller has no cancel right under this rider (Seller needs Rider X to keep marketing and replace the Buyer).
- **Money Effects:** None directly. Buyer's cash to close usually depends on the proceeds of the other sale; if the contingency lapses the Buyer must fund closing without them.
- **Changes to the Contract:** Adds a contingency. Para. 8(b)(i): a Loan Approval that requires Buyer to sell other real property does not count as Loan Approval unless Rider V is attached; with Rider V attached, a sale-conditioned loan approval does count. The rider does not move the Para. 4 Closing Date; a sale date set after the Closing Date conflicts (ask).
- **Red Flags:** Date blank or after the Closing Date. "Is/is not under contract" unchecked. Buyer's property "is" under contract but no redacted copy delivered within 24 hours. No Rider X attached (Seller is off the market with no exit). The rider only requires the other sale to close; it gives Seller no right to end the deal if Buyer never lists the property. Existing `frbar.md` note uses a 30-day default for `sale_contingency_days`: the rider has no day default; it is a date the parties fill in.
- **Skill Use:** Timeline: add "24 hours after offer delivery: redacted copy of Buyer's sale contract" (when "is" is checked), "Buyer's sale must close by <date>", and "<date> + 3 days: Buyer's last day to cancel; after this the contingency ends". Seller offer review: count the Deposit as refundable through date + 3 days, certainty low (lower if Buyer's property "is not" under contract), and recommend adding Rider X. Buyer offer builder: warn that this makes the offer weak in competition; if used, recommend the date match the other contract's closing plus margin, and warn that missing the 3-day notice window puts the Deposit at risk.

### W. Back-Up Contract Rider

**Form:** `CR-7_W`, CR-7 Rev. 10/21. **Allowed On:** Both forms.

- **Purpose:** Makes this a back-up contract, subject to termination of Seller's prior executed contract with a third party. It moves into first position only if Seller delivers written notice of that termination by a set date.
- **Blanks and Defaults:** Date by which Seller must deliver notice of the prior contract's termination: no default (ask).
- **Deadlines:**
  - On or before the filled-in date: Seller delivers written notice that the prior contract terminated. Result: the contingency is removed, this contract moves into first position, and its Effective Date becomes the date Seller delivers that notice. If missed: the rider does not say what happens; the contingency is not removed. Safe reading: the back-up does not become primary and Buyer should terminate in writing or the parties should agree in writing to extend or end it (ask the agent).
  - Before Seller delivers that notice: Buyer may terminate by written notice. If missed: once notice is delivered, Buyer is in first position and bound by all contract terms.
- **Cancel Rights:** Buyer, by written notice any time before Seller delivers written notice of the prior contract's termination. Deposit refunded "if any Deposit(s) have been paid". Seller has no cancel right under this rider.
- **Money Effects:** None directly. Deposit timing follows Para. 2: an initial deposit that "accompanies offer" is held in escrow now; deposits due "N days after Effective Date" become due N days after Seller's notice (the new Effective Date).
- **Changes to the Contract:** Overrides Para. 3(b): the Effective Date is the date Seller delivers the termination notice, not the date of the last signature. Every period counted from the Effective Date (deposits, Para. 8 loan application and Loan Approval Period, Para. 12 Inspection Period, Para. 6(b), Para. 10(d), rider periods) restarts from that notice. The Para. 4 Closing Date is a fixed date and does not move; check it still leaves room.
- **Red Flags:** Date blank. Closing Date that cannot work if the back-up moves up late. Initial deposit made to escrow up front while Buyer may still walk (refundable, but tied up). Buyer keeps signing other offers: Buyer can cancel only until Seller's notice arrives. A back-up offer that also includes a kick-out (Rider X) on the primary deal: confirm the pairing.
- **Skill Use:** Timeline: show all dates as "not started" until Seller's termination notice; then compute every Effective Date period from the notice date; add the notice deadline date. Seller offer review: rank as a back-up only; certainty and net apply only if the primary fails; note Buyer can walk until notice. Buyer offer builder: recommend a short notice date, deposit timing "N days after Effective Date" rather than with the offer, and a Closing Date far enough out; warn that once notice arrives all deadlines start at once.

### X. Kick-Out Clause Rider

**Form:** `CR-7_X`, CR-7 Rev. 10/21. **Allowed On:** Both forms.

- **Purpose:** Lets Seller keep showing the Property and sign bona fide back-up contracts. When Seller does, Buyer must either post an additional deposit within 3 days (waiving the financing and sale-of-property contingencies) or lose the contract.
- **Blanks and Defaults:** Amount of the additional deposit: no default (ask). The 3-day response period is preprinted.
- **Deadlines:**
  - Any time during the Contract (no end date stated): Seller may enter into a bona fide back-up contract subject to termination of this Contract, then gives Buyer a copy with the back-up buyer's identity and price blacked out. This copy is the trigger.
  - 3 days from Buyer's receipt of the back-up contract copy (receipt by Buyer's agent counts, Standard O): Buyer delivers the additional deposit to Escrow Agent. Doing so waives all contingencies for financing and sale of Buyer's property, and the parties close on the Closing Date. If missed: this Contract terminates automatically and the Deposit is refunded to Buyer.
- **Cancel Rights:** No notice is needed from Seller: failure to deposit on time terminates the Contract automatically, with the Deposit refunded to Buyer. Buyer can in effect choose to let the Contract end by not depositing.
- **Money Effects:** The additional deposit goes to Escrow Agent and is credited to Buyer at Closing (it reduces Buyer's remaining cash to close, not the price). After the waiver the whole Deposit is at risk if Buyer cannot close for loan or sale reasons (Para. 15(a)). No effect on Seller's net.
- **Changes to the Contract:** Adds Seller's right to keep marketing. On a timely additional deposit, removes the Para. 8(b) financing contingency (Buyer proceeds as if cash, Para. 8(a)) and the Rider V sale contingency. The rider does not waive the inspection period (Para. 12), title, or other riders. Whether "financing" includes a separate Appraisal Contingency Rider (F) or the FHA/VA rider's appraisal clause is not stated; safe reading: treat Rider F as not waived and ask the agent, and note the FHA/VA amendatory clause cannot be waived by the buyer.
- **Red Flags:** Additional deposit amount blank (Buyer cannot comply; ask). Delivery of the back-up copy not documented (the 3-day clock starts on receipt). A "back-up" that is only an offer, not an executed back-up contract. Buyer who needs a loan: keeping the contract means waiving the loan contingency. Pair with Rider V: the kick-out is the Seller's protection against a long sale contingency.
- **Skill Use:** Timeline: add an event-driven row "Receipt of back-up contract + 3 days: Buyer deposits $X or the Contract ends"; once deposited, remove the Loan Approval and sale-of-property rows and mark the Deposit at risk. Seller offer review: count as a certainty improver for a contingent offer (Seller can replace the Buyer) and note the additional deposit amount; net unchanged. Buyer offer builder: warn the buyer must be able to waive financing and the sale contingency and fund the extra deposit within 3 days; recommend the smallest deposit the seller accepts.

### CC. Miami-Dade County Special Taxing District Disclosure Rider

**Form:** `CR-7_CC`, CR-7 Rev. 10/21. **Allowed On:** Both forms.

By initialing, the parties acknowledge Buyer got the disclosure on the same date the Contract was signed. Required for a seller of "residential property" whose prior year tax bill showed a special taxing district assessment, or a seller of "new residential property" when a district exists or a petition to create one is pending (terms as defined in s. 18-20.2).

- **Cancel Rights:** None in the rider.

Which applies to a given Miami-Dade district is not stated in the rider; ask the closing agent.
- **Changes to the Contract:** Adds a disclosure; changes no paragraph. Works with Para. 9(f) and Standard K for proration.
- **Red Flags:** Property in Miami-Dade with a non-ad valorem district charge on the prior year bill and no Rider CC. District name or service type blank. Rider initialed after the Contract date. New construction with a pending district petition not disclosed.
- **Skill Use:** Timeline: no row. Seller offer review: no net effect beyond ordinary proration; flag a missing rider in Miami-Dade as a compliance item. Buyer offer builder: include the district charge in the buyer's monthly tax estimate (labeled from the tax bill) and attach Rider CC for Miami-Dade listings with a district charge.

### DD. Seasonal and Vacation Rentals After Closing Rider

**Form:** `CR-7_DD`, CR-7 Rev. 10/21. **Allowed On:** Both forms.

- **Purpose:** For a Property that is or may be rented seasonally or short-term, sets whether Seller may book new or renewal rentals for stays after Closing, and gives Buyer copies of, and a right to reject, any Property Management Agreements behind existing bookings.
- **Blanks and Defaults:** Paragraph A, Seller "may" or "may not" enter new or renewal seasonal or vacation rental agreements after Effective Date for stays after Closing: no default (ask). If "may": check "only with Buyer's prior written approval" or "without Buyer's prior approval": no default (ask). The 5-day periods in Paragraph B are preprinted.
- **Deadlines:**
  - At or before Closing: Seller gives Buyer copies of any new or renewal occupancy agreements signed after Effective Date for stays after Closing. If missed: no stated remedy; ask.
  - 5 days after Effective Date: if existing seasonal bookings came through a Property Management Agreement, Seller gives Buyer copies of all such agreements. If missed: no stated remedy in the rider; Buyer's 5-day review period below has not started.
  - 5 days after Buyer receives the Property Management Agreement(s): Buyer may terminate under Para. 6(b) if the terms are not acceptable; Deposit refunded. If missed: Buyer is bound by the management agreement(s) for the length of the bookings in place at Closing (survives Closing).
- **Cancel Rights:** Buyer, by written notice within 5 days after receiving the Property Management Agreement(s), under Para. 6(b). Deposit refunded to Buyer. Para. 6(b) separately gives Buyer 5 days after receiving the lease disclosures and copies (due 5 days after Effective Date) to reject the occupancy terms.
- **Money Effects:** The rider itself sets no rent split. Under Standard K, rents are prorated as of the day before Closing and advance rent and security deposits are credited to Buyer; for bookings spanning Closing this moves rent collected by Seller to Buyer on the settlement statement. Tenant estoppel letters are not required for seasonal or short-term rentals (Para. 6(b)); Seller's affidavit under Standard D still applies. After Closing Buyer pays any management fees under a surviving Property Management Agreement. Rental taxes and platform-held booking deposits are not addressed; ask the closing agent.
- **Changes to the Contract:** Works with Para. 6(b) (check it) and Standard D and K. Limits or allows Seller's right to create new post-Closing occupancies, which Para. 6(a) would otherwise bar (possession free of tenants and future tenancies unless 6(b) is checked). Adds Buyer's review and termination right for Property Management Agreements; binds Buyer to them after Closing (survives Closing).
- **Red Flags:** "May/may not" unchecked, or "may" checked with no approval method. Para. 6(b) not checked on a rental property. Bookings deep into the future at below-market rates. Property Management Agreement copies not delivered by day 5. Buyer who intends to occupy on a date that conflicts with existing bookings. Advance rent and damage deposits held by a manager or booking platform, not Seller (confirm who credits them).
- **Skill Use:** Timeline: add "Day 5: Seller delivers Property Management Agreements", "receipt + 5 days: Buyer's last day to cancel over management terms", the Para. 6(b) rows, and "at or before Closing: copies of new bookings". Seller offer review: count advance rents and deposits credited to Buyer at Closing as reducing Seller's cash; count Buyer's 5-day review right as a certainty item. Buyer offer builder: recommend "may, only with Buyer's prior written approval" or "may not", and warn the buyer will be bound by the manager's agreement for bookings in place at Closing.

### EE. Qualifying Improvements Rider

**Form:** `CR-7_EE`, CR-7x Rev. 2/26. **Allowed On:** Both forms.

- **Purpose:** Gives the seller's written disclosure required by s. 163.081(8), F.S., when a non-ad valorem assessment for a qualifying improvement (the program formerly known as PACE) has been levied on the Property and has an unpaid balance. It explains that the debt is repaid through annual non-ad valorem assessments on the property tax bill and prints the statutory disclosure statement. Formerly the PACE (Property Assessed Clean Energy) Disclosure Rider; the contracts' Para. 19 checklist lists it as "Qualifying Improvements Disclosure", and the form heading reads "Qualifying Improvements Disclosure".
- **Blanks and Defaults:** Party names and property description (header); all parties initial. No amount, payoff or assumption blanks.
- **Deadlines:** None in the rider. By statute the disclosure is due at or before the time Seller signs the Contract.
- **Cancel Rights:** None in the rider.
- **Money Effects:** The rider does not say who pays the unpaid balance. By default the assessment stays with the Property and is paid through the annual tax bill, raising Buyer's carrying cost. Whether Para. 9(f) makes Seller pay it (as a certified public-body lien) or treats it as installments prorated under option (a) or prepaid under option (b), or whether Standard K proration applies, is not stated; safe reading: get the payoff figure and the annual installment, and write payoff or assumption into Para. 20 Additional Terms. Buyer's lender may require payoff before closing (ask the lender); a payoff reduces Seller's net by the payoff amount.
- **Changes to the Contract:** Adds the statutory disclosure; changes no paragraph. Money handling falls back to Para. 9(f) and Standard K unless Additional Terms say otherwise.
- **Red Flags:** A qualifying improvement or PACE charge on the tax bill with no Rider EE, or the rider initialed after Seller signed. No written agreement on payoff vs. assumption. Buyer's loan program not checked for a payoff requirement. The form cites "section 168.08(4)(a)" for the list of qualifying improvements; that section number appears to be a misprint for Chapter 163 (confirm with the current statute; do not repeat the citation).
- **Skill Use:** Timeline: no statutory row; if payoff is agreed, add "payoff statement ordered" before Closing. Seller offer review: if the balance must be paid at Closing, subtract the payoff from net; if unresolved, flag it as a money risk and certainty item (lender may refuse the loan). Buyer offer builder: include the annual assessment in the buyer's monthly cost, ask the lender whether payoff is required, and recommend Additional Terms that Seller pays off the balance at or before Closing.

### FF. Credit Related to Buyer's Broker Compensation Rider

**Form:** `CR-7_FF`, CR-7 Rev. 8/24. **Allowed On:** Both forms.

- **Purpose:** Seller agrees to give Buyer a credit at Closing that, for disclosure purposes, the parties agree relates to Buyer's obligation to pay Buyer's broker under Buyer's separate brokerage agreement.
- **Blanks and Defaults:** Buyer Broker (brokerage name): no default (ask). Credit amount, check one: percent of Purchase Price; a dollar amount; or percent of Purchase Price plus a dollar amount: no default (ask). If the credit exceeds the lender's limit, check one: the balance is paid by Seller directly to Buyer Broker, or the credit is reduced to the lender's maximum: if not checked, the balance is paid by Seller directly to Buyer Broker.
- **Deadlines:** None.
- **Cancel Rights:** None.
- **Money Effects:**
  - Seller credits Buyer the stated sum at Closing (a seller credit to Buyer on the settlement statement); it reduces Seller's net and Buyer's cash to close.
  - Lender cap: if the credit exceeds what Buyer's lender allows for such contributions, then, to the extent the lender permits, either the excess is paid by Seller directly to Buyer Broker (default: Seller's net still falls by the full amount), or the credit is cut to the lender's maximum (Seller's net falls by less).
  - Buyer Broker Agreement cap: if the credit exceeds what the Buyer Broker Agreement allows, the credit is reduced to that amount, so the credit never exceeds what Buyer owes Buyer Broker.
  - Buyer still owes Buyer Broker any part of the fee not covered by the credit, in cash at Closing.
- **Changes to the Contract:** Adds a Seller credit at Closing. Does not change the Buyer Broker Agreement or any other compensation agreement among Seller, Buyer and the brokers, and does not require anyone to show the Buyer Broker Agreement to Seller or Seller's broker. Works alongside the contract's broker section, which directs the Closing Agent to pay brokerage fees under separate agreements and does not modify any offer of compensation by Seller or the listing broker.
- **Red Flags:** No box checked for the amount. Credit plus other seller concessions over the loan program's interested-party contribution limit (confirm with the lender; the default sends the excess to Buyer Broker). Buyer Broker Agreement fee lower than the credit (credit shrinks). The same compensation also promised in Rider GG or in a separate agreement (risk of paying twice; read them together). Percent applied to a price later changed by amendment.
- **Skill Use:** Timeline: no row. Seller offer review: subtract the full credit from net (label it "Buyer broker credit"), and under the default count the full amount even if the lender caps it; compare offers on net after this credit. Buyer offer builder: compute the credit from the Buyer Broker Agreement fee, check it against the loan program's concession cap together with any closing cost credit, and choose the lender-cap option deliberately.

### GG. Seller's Agreement with Respect to Buyer's Broker Compensation Rider

**Form:** `CR-7_GG`, CR-7 Rev. 8/24. **Allowed On:** Both forms.

- **Purpose:** Makes the Contract contingent on a separate compensation agreement for Buyer's broker being signed within a short period, with terms acceptable to Buyer. The amount is not in the rider; it lives in the separate Compensation Agreement.
- **Blanks and Defaults:** Who signs the Compensation Agreement with Buyer's Broker, check one: Seller's Broker, or Seller: no default (ask). Time Period: 3 days after Effective Date if left blank. No amount or percent blank.
- **Deadlines:**
  - N days (default 3) after Effective Date: the Compensation Agreement is executed and delivered. If missed: Buyer may terminate (next row).
  - 3 days after the Time Period ends: Buyer may deliver written notice terminating the Contract; Deposit refunded. If missed: the contingency ends ("no further force or effect") and Buyer proceeds without the agreement.
- **Cancel Rights:** Buyer only, by written notice within 3 days after the Time Period if the Compensation Agreement was not executed and delivered in time. Deposit refunded. Because the terms must be "acceptable to Buyer", Buyer largely controls whether this contingency is met.
- **Money Effects:** The amount and how it is paid come from the separate Compensation Agreement. At Closing the contract's broker section instructs the Closing Agent to pay brokerage fees per the separate brokerage and cooperative agreements, so the payment usually appears as a Seller-paid (or listing-broker-paid) commission line, not a credit to Buyer. If Seller signs it, Seller's net falls by that amount; if Seller's Broker signs it, the listing broker usually pays from its own fee (check the listing agreement).
- **Changes to the Contract:** Adds a Buyer-side contingency and cancel right. Does not itself state or change any compensation; the contract's broker section still says it does not modify any offer of compensation.
- **Red Flags:** Signer box unchecked. Compensation Agreement not in the file by day 3. The same compensation also given as a credit under Rider FF (double counting). Amount unknown to Seller at acceptance: net cannot be computed until the agreement is seen.
- **Skill Use:** Timeline: add "Day N (default 3): compensation agreement signed and delivered" and "Day N + 3: Buyer's last day to cancel if it was not". The 3 days count from the Time Period's end as extended past a weekend or holiday (the later, buyer-favorable date); when it rolled, give the agent the reading from the unextended day too. Seller offer review: ask for the Compensation Agreement amount; subtract it from net if Seller pays; treat the Deposit as refundable until day N + 3 and certainty as lower until the agreement is signed. Buyer offer builder: have the Compensation Agreement drafted and ready to sign with the offer so the contingency clears at once; choose GG (fee paid to the broker) or FF (credit to the buyer), not both for the same money.
