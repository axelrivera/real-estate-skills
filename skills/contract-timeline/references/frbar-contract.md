# FR/BAR Residential Contracts

This file covers the two Florida Realtors/Florida Bar residential purchase contracts, the AS IS form and the Standard form: every paragraph, standard, blank, default, deadline and cost split, and every place the two forms differ. Read it whenever you analyze, summarize or build a timeline from an executed or proposed FR/BAR contract. Paragraph and line numbers are from the revisions in the table below; check the footer of the contract you're reading, and if its revision differs, tell the agent these rules were checked against the revisions listed here and that numbers or terms may have moved.

|---|---|
| `FRBAR-ASIS` | FloridaRealtors/FloridaBar-ASIS-7x Rev. 2/26 |
| `FRBAR-STANDARD` | FloridaRealtors/FloridaBar – 7x Rev. 2/26 |

The AS IS form has 13 pages (lines 1–652); the Standard form has 14 pages (lines 1–735). Line numbers below are the printed margin numbers.

## Telling the Forms Apart

Identify the form from its own text, never from the listing, the agent's description or the deal's feel. The two forms run different inspection, repair and cancel rules, so a wrong guess changes the math.

| Clue | AS IS | Standard |
|---|---|---|
| Title (top of page 1) | "AS IS" Residential Contract For Sale And Purchase | Residential Contract For Sale And Purchase |
| Footer | FloridaRealtors/FloridaBar-ASIS-7x Rev. 2/26 | FloridaRealtors/FloridaBar – 7x Rev. 2/26 |
| Page count | 13 | 14 |
| Para. 9(a) | No repair limit blanks | General Repair, WDO Repair and Permit Limit blanks (lines 143–150) |
| Para. 12 heading | Property Inspection; Right to Cancel | Property Inspection and Repair |
| Last standard | X (Buyer Waiver of Claims); W is RESERVED | V (FIRPTA); there is no W or X |
| Para. 19 riders I, K, L | RESERVED | Mold Inspection, "As Is", Right to Inspect/Cancel |

Rules:

- **Never guess the form.** If the title and footer are missing (a partial copy, a cropped scan), use the clues above; if they still don't settle it, ask the agent. Never default a missing form silently: ask, or record it as a high-impact assumption.
- **A Standard form with Rider K ("As Is") or Rider L (Right to Inspect/Cancel) checked is still the Standard form**, changed by a rider whose text is not in this file. The rider controls where it conflicts (Standard R). Ask for the rider and read it; don't swap in the AS IS rules.
- **Not these forms:** the Florida Realtors-only "Contract for Residential Sale and Purchase" (CRSP, e.g. CRSP-17) is a different form, not approved by The Florida Bar, with its own paragraph order and terms. The Vacant Land Contract, commercial contracts and condominium or new-construction contracts are also different forms. None of the paragraph numbers or defaults here apply to them; read their own text.
- **Older revisions** (earlier than Rev. 2/26) may have different paragraphs or blanks (for example, Standard I(iii) FinCEN reporting is new for transactions from March 1, 2026). Say which revision you checked against.

## Paragraph by Paragraph

Each entry gives the scope, what the paragraph sets, its blanks and defaults, deadlines, cancel rights and costs. "ED" means Effective Date.

### Parties

**Both, Different Text.** Lines 1–5. Names Seller and Buyer and defines "Property" (Real Property plus Personal Property) and "Contract" (the form plus riders and addenda). The only difference is the form name in line 4 ("AS IS Residential Contract..." vs. "Residential Contract..."). No defaults: a missing party name is a red flag.

### 1. Property Description

**Both.** Lines 6–25.

- 1(a) street address, city, zip; 1(b) county and property tax ID; 1(c) legal description. No defaults.
- 1(c): Real Property includes existing improvements and fixtures, built-in appliances, built-in furnishings and attached wall-to-wall carpeting and flooring, unless excluded in 1(e) or elsewhere.
- 1(d): the printed Personal Property list is included if owned by Seller and on the Property as of the date of the initial offer: ranges/ovens, refrigerators, dishwashers, disposal, ceiling fans, light fixtures, drapery rods and draperies, blinds, window treatments, smoke detectors, garage door openers, thermostats, doorbells, TV wall mounts and mounting hardware, security gate and other access devices, mailbox keys, storm shutters and storm protection items and hardware. A blank line lists other included items.
- Personal Property is included in the price, has "no contributory value", and must be left for Buyer.
- 1(e): excluded items. Blank means nothing beyond the printed list is excluded.

### 2. Purchase Price and Closing

**Both.** Lines 27–42.

- Purchase Price in U.S. currency.
- 2(a) initial deposit (checks subject to Collection), payable to the Escrow Agent named in the blanks (name, address, phone, email, fax). Check one: (i) accompanies offer, or (ii) within a blank number of days after ED. **If left blank, 3 days. If neither box is checked, option (ii) is deemed selected.**
- 2(b) additional deposit within a blank number of days after ED. **If left blank, 10 days.** A blank amount means no additional deposit.
- All deposits paid or agreed to be paid are "the Deposit".
- 2(c) financing: Loan Amount as a dollar amount or percentage (see Para. 8). The form doesn't say what a percentage is of; the usual reading is the Purchase Price. Confirm if it matters.
- 2(d) other; 2(e) balance to close by wire or other Collected funds (Standard S), not counting Buyer's closing costs, prepaids and prorations.
- The form doesn't state that 2(a) through 2(e) must add up to the price, but they are meant to. A mismatch is a red flag.
- Missing a deposit is a Buyer default (Para. 15(a) names "payment of the Deposit").

### 3. Time for Acceptance; Effective Date

**Both.** Lines 43–49.

- 3(a): unless signed by Buyer and Seller and an executed copy is delivered to all parties on or before the written date, the offer is withdrawn and any Deposit returned to Buyer. **No default for the date.** Counter-offers must be accepted within 2 days after the day the counter-offer is delivered, unless otherwise stated.
- 3(b): the Effective Date is when the last of Buyer and Seller has signed or initialed **and delivered** the offer or final counter-offer. No default: if the ED isn't written or can't be fixed from signatures and delivery, ask.
- Standard F's weekend and holiday rollover does **not** apply to the acceptance time or the Effective Date.

### 4. Closing; Closing Date

**Both.** Lines 50–54. Closing happens when all closing funds are received and Collected (Standard S) and all required closing documents are delivered. The Closing Date is written in the blank; the time is set by the Closing Agent. **No default date.** The Closing Date is subject to Standard F rollover and to extensions in Para. 5, Standard A(ii), Standard G and Standard 12(d)(ii) (Standard form only).

### 5. Extension of Closing Date

**Both.** Lines 55–63.

- 5(a) CFPB: if lender funds aren't available on the Closing Date because of Closing Disclosure delivery rules, and Para. 8(b) is checked, Loan Approval has been obtained and underwriting is complete, the Closing Date extends as needed, **up to 7 days**.
- 5(b) Force Majeure: if an event makes services essential to Closing unavailable (including utilities or hazard, wind, flood or homeowners' insurance), the Closing Date extends per Standard G.

### 6. Occupancy and Possession

**Both, Different Text.** AS IS lines 64–82; Standard lines 64–83.

- 6(a): unless 6(b) is checked, Seller delivers occupancy and possession at Closing free of tenants, occupants and future tenancies, with personal items and trash removed and all keys, garage door openers, access devices and codes delivered. If Buyer takes occupancy before Closing, Buyer takes all risk of loss and maintenance from that date and accepts the Property in its existing condition (see Rider T).
  - **Standard only** (lines 71–72): the acceptance on early occupancy excludes items Buyer identified under Para. 12 before taking occupancy that require repair, replacement, treatment or remedy.
- 6(b) checkbox: Property subject to leases or occupancy after Closing (including seasonal and short-term rentals). Seller discloses the facts and terms in writing and delivers copies of written leases **within 5 days after ED**. Buyer may terminate, in Buyer's sole discretion, by written notice **within 5 days after receipt** of those items; Deposit refunded. Estoppel letters and Seller's affidavit follow Standard D, but tenant estoppels aren't required for seasonal or short-term rentals. If Seller will occupy after Closing, see Rider U.
- The form doesn't say what happens if Seller misses the 5-day disclosure. Treat it as a Seller obligation not yet met and ask the agent.

### 7. Assignability

**Both.** AS IS lines 83–85; Standard lines 84–86. Check one: Buyer may assign and be released; may assign but not be released; or may not assign. **If no box is checked, Buyer may not assign.**

### 8. Financing

**Both, Different Text** (wording only: AS IS line 126 cites "8b(iii)", Standard line 127 "8(b)(iii)", and Standard line 113 adds "the"; the rules are the same). AS IS lines 87–133; Standard lines 88–134.

- 8(a): cash, no financing contingency.
- 8(b): contingent on, within the **Loan Approval Period** (blank days after ED, **if left blank, 30**): (1) approval of a conventional, FHA, VA or other loan (check one; no default) with fixed, adjustable, or fixed or adjustable rate (check one; no default), in the Loan Amount from 2(c), at an initial rate not over the blank (**if left blank, prevailing rate based on Buyer's creditworthiness**), for a term of the blank (**if left blank, 30 years**); and (2) the lender receiving an appraisal or alternative valuation, if the lender requires one, sufficient for the lender to fund ("Appraisal").
- 8(b)(i): Buyer applies **within the blank days after ED (if left blank, 5)** and uses good faith and diligent effort to get Loan Approval within the Loan Approval Period, then to close. Approval that requires selling other property isn't Loan Approval unless Rider V is attached. Failing to use good faith and diligent effort is a Buyer default. "Diligent effort" includes timely furnishing documents and paying for the Appraisal and application fees.
- 8(b)(ii): on written request, Buyer keeps Seller and Broker informed; Buyer authorizes lender, broker and Closing Agent to share status and closing disclosures.
- 8(b)(iii): before the Loan Approval Period ends, Buyer gives written notice either that Loan Approval was obtained, or that Buyer is satisfied Buyer can get it and close.
- 8(b)(iv): if Buyer can't get Loan Approval (or can't meet its terms) after good faith effort, Buyer may terminate by written notice **before the Loan Approval Period ends**; Deposit refunded if Buyer isn't in default.
- 8(b)(v): if Buyer gives neither notice in time, Buyer proceeds **as if 8(a) (cash) had been checked** from the ED. Seller may terminate by written notice **within 3 days after the Loan Approval Period ends**; Deposit refunded to Buyer if Buyer isn't in default.
- 8(b)(vi): if Buyer gave an 8(b)(iii) notice and then fails to close, Seller gets the Deposit, unless the failure is due to Seller default or an unmet contingency, or to Property-related loan conditions not met (the Appraisal valuation is specifically excluded) that weren't waived elsewhere; then Buyer gets the Deposit back.
- 8(c) assumption of existing mortgage (Rider D); 8(d) purchase money mortgage to Seller (Rider C).
- The form gives no default when neither 8(a) nor 8(b) is checked. Ask.

### 9. Closing Costs; Title Insurance; Survey; Home Warranty; Special Assessments

**Both, Different Text** (9(a) differs). AS IS lines 135–202; Standard lines 136–218.

- **9(a) Seller pays (both forms):** documentary stamp taxes and surtax on the deed; HOA/condominium estoppel fees; Owner's Policy and Charges if 9(c)(i) is checked; recording and other fees to cure title; title search charges if 9(c)(iii); Seller's attorneys' fees; municipal lien search if 9(c)(i) or (iii); Seller's Closing Services; FIRPTA withholding and reporting charges; other (blank).
- **9(a) AS IS only** (lines 142–145): if Seller can't meet the AS IS Maintenance Requirement (Para. 11) before Closing, 125% of the estimated cost is escrowed at Closing; Seller pays any actual cost above the escrow; unused escrow returns to Seller. No cap.
- **9(a) Standard only** (lines 143–157): Seller pays up to a dollar amount or percentage of the Purchase Price, **each 1.5% if left blank**, for (i) General Repair Items ("General Repair Limit"), (ii) WDO treatment and repairs ("WDO Repair Limit"), and (iii) closing out open or expired permits and obtaining permits for unpermitted existing improvements ("Permit Limit"). If Seller can't finish the Maintenance Requirement or the Para. 12 repairs, treatments or permitting before Closing, 125% of the estimated cost is escrowed, **capped at the applicable limits**; Seller pays actual costs above the escrow, still capped at the limits; unused escrow returns to Seller.
- **9(b) Buyer pays (both forms):** taxes and recording fees on notes and mortgages; recording fees for the deed and financing statements; Owner's Policy and Charges if 9(c)(ii); survey and elevation certificate if required; lender's title policy and endorsements; HOA/condominium application and transfer fees; municipal lien search if 9(c)(ii); other (blank); loan expenses; appraisal fees; Buyer's inspections; Buyer's attorneys' fees; all property-related insurance; owner's policy premium if 9(c)(iii); Buyer's Closing Services.
- **9(c) Title evidence:** the Title Commitment (from a Florida-licensed title insurer, with legible copies of exception documents) is due **at least the blank days before the Closing Date ("Title Evidence Deadline"); if left blank, 15, or 5 if 8(a) (cash) is checked.** The form uses the passive voice ("shall be obtained and delivered to Buyer"); in practice the Closing Agent of the party who designates it under 9(c) produces it. If Seller has an owner's policy or other title evidence, Seller furnishes a copy to Buyer and Closing Agent **within 5 days after ED**. Premiums are calculated and allocated under Florida law but may be shown differently on federal disclosures. Each party pays its own Closing Services (s. 627.7711(1)(a), F.S.). "Municipal lien search" means the search needed to insure without exception for unrecorded governmental liens under Chapters 153, 159 or 170, F.S.
  - Check one: (i) Seller designates Closing Agent and pays Owner's Policy and Charges; Buyer pays the lender's policy and endorsements. (ii) Buyer designates Closing Agent and pays Owner's Policy and Charges and the lender's policy. (iii) Miami-Dade/Broward regional provision: Buyer designates Closing Agent and pays the owner's and lender's premiums, endorsements and any post-Closing continuation; Seller pays actual costs of (A) title search or continuation, **not over the blank (if left blank, $200.00)**, (B) tax search and (C) municipal lien search.
  - **No default if no 9(c) box is checked.** Standard I(i) says Closing Agent is designated by whoever pays for the owner's policy, and Seller designates if there's no title insurance, but that doesn't pick a box. Ask; don't fill it from local custom without saying so.
- **9(d) Survey:** Buyer may, at Buyer's expense, have a Florida-registered surveyor survey and certify the Real Property **at least 5 days before the Closing Date**. If Seller has a survey, Seller furnishes a copy to Buyer and Closing Agent **within 5 days after ED**. Survey problems follow Standard B.
- **9(e) Home warranty:** Buyer, Seller or N/A pays at Closing for a plan from the named provider, at a cost not over the blank. **No default**; if nothing is checked, ask (the safe reading is that no one is obligated).
- **9(f) Special assessments:** at Closing Seller pays (i) public-body liens (not an HOA or condo association) certified, confirmed and ratified before Closing, and (ii) the public body's latest estimate or assessment for an improvement substantially complete as of ED that hasn't become a lien before Closing. Buyer pays all other assessments. If payable in installments, check one: (a) Seller pays installments due before Closing, Buyer those due after, with the Closing-year installment prorated; or (b) Seller prepays in full any assessment the public body allows to be prepaid (option (a) applies to any that can't be). **If neither box is checked, option (a).** 9(f) doesn't apply to CDD special benefit tax liens (Chapter 190, F.S.) or special district assessments (Chapter 189, F.S.); those are prorated under Standard K.

### 10. Disclosures

**Both, Different Text** (10(c) and 10(j) differ). AS IS lines 204–255; Standard lines 220–270.

- 10(a) Radon: statutory notice.
- 10(b) Permits: Seller knows of no unpermitted improvements or open permits except as disclosed in writing; if Seller identifies any, Seller promptly delivers plans and documents in Seller's possession.
- 10(c) Mold: advisory. **Standard only** (lines 233–234) adds "See Rider I MOLD INSPECTION" (Rider I is RESERVED on the AS IS form).
- 10(d) Flood zone; elevation: Buyer is advised to verify by elevation certificate. If the Property is in a Special Flood Hazard Area, a Coastal Barrier Resources Act area or other protected area, **and** the lowest floor is below minimum flood elevation or the Property can't get NFIP or private flood insurance (42 U.S.C. 4012a), Buyer may terminate by written notice **within the blank days after ED (if left blank, 20)**, Deposit refunded; otherwise Buyer accepts the existing elevation and flood zone designation.
- 10(e) Buyer acknowledges receipt of the Florida Energy-Efficiency Rating brochure (s. 553.996, F.S.).
- 10(f) Lead-based paint disclosure is mandatory for pre-1978 housing.
- 10(g) Buyer should not sign until receiving and reading the HOA/community disclosure, if applicable.
- 10(h) Property tax disclosure summary: don't rely on Seller's current taxes; a sale or improvements trigger reassessment.
- 10(i) FIRPTA: Seller tells Buyer in writing if Seller is a "foreign person"; parties comply (may require more cash from Seller); a non-foreign Seller can give a certification at or before Closing. See Standard V.
- 10(j) Seller disclosure (both): Seller knows of no facts materially affecting value that aren't readily observable and haven't been disclosed; Seller has received no notice of uncorrected building, environmental or safety code violations except as disclosed in writing. **AS IS only** (lines 251–253): except for that sentence, Seller gives no warranty or representation of any kind about the physical condition or history of the Property.

### 11. Property Maintenance

**Both, Different Text.** AS IS lines 257–259; Standard lines 272–275. Except for ordinary wear and tear and Casualty Loss, Seller maintains the Property (including lawn, shrubbery and pool) in its condition as of ED.

- AS IS names this the "AS IS Maintenance Requirement".
- Standard names it the "Maintenance Requirement" and also excepts the repairs, replacements or treatments the contract requires.
- Escrow if unmet: Para. 9(a) (uncapped on AS IS, capped by the limits on Standard; see the note in Side by Side).

### 12. Property Inspection

**Both, Different Text.** This is the main difference between the forms. AS IS lines 260–291 (12(a)–(d)); Standard lines 276–384 (12(a)–(f)).

**AS IS 12(a) Property Inspections and Right to Cancel** (lines 261–274): Buyer has **the blank days after ED (if left blank, 15)** ("Inspection Period") for any inspections Buyer wants. If Buyer decides, in Buyer's sole discretion, the Property isn't acceptable, Buyer may terminate by written notice **before the Inspection Period expires**; the Deposit is returned. Buyer must promptly pay for inspections, repair and restore any damage from them, and give Seller paid receipts (this survives termination). If Buyer doesn't terminate, Buyer accepts the physical condition and any code, building, environmental or safety violations, subject to Seller's continuing AS IS Maintenance Requirement, and Buyer is responsible for all repairs and improvements Buyer's lender requires.

**AS IS 12(b) Walk-through** (lines 275–279): on the day before the Closing Date, or on the Closing Date before Closing, as Buyer specifies, Buyer or a representative may walk through (and follow up) solely to confirm Personal Property is there, the AS IS Maintenance Requirement is met and other obligations are met.

**AS IS 12(c) Seller assistance with building permits** (lines 280–288): if Buyer's inspection finds open or needed permits, Seller promptly delivers plans and documents and cooperates in good faith (including signing authorizations and consents) so Buyer can inspect and get estimates, but Seller need not spend or commit any money.

**AS IS 12(d) Assignment of contracts and warranties** (lines 289–291): at Buyer's option and cost, Seller assigns assignable repair, treatment and maintenance contracts and warranties at Closing.

**Standard 12(a) Inspection Period** (lines 277–284): Buyer has **the blank days after ED (if left blank, 15)**, at Buyer's expense, for General, WDO and Permit Inspections. **There is no walk-away for any reason.** If Buyer doesn't deliver a required notice or report under 12(b), (c) or (d) in time, Buyer waives Seller's obligations to repair, replace, treat or remedy anything not inspected and timely reported (the Maintenance Requirement continues). If the contract doesn't close, Buyer repairs inspection damage, restores the Property and gives Seller paid receipts.

**Standard 12(b) General inspection and repair** (lines 285–324):

- (i) General Repair Items may be inspected by a licensed home inspector or a Florida-licensed tradesperson ("Professional Inspector"). Buyer gives written notice of items not in the required condition **within the Inspection Period**, and on Seller's written request a copy of the relevant report pages.
- (ii) Required condition: ceiling, roof (including fascia and soffits), exterior and interior walls, doors, windows and foundation free of leaks, water damage or structural damage; those items plus pool, pool equipment, non-leased major appliances, heating, cooling, mechanical, electrical, security, sprinkler, septic and plumbing systems, seawalls, dockage and watercraft lifts in "Working Condition" (operating as designed) through Closing. Seller must fix torn screens (including pool and patio), fogged windows and missing roof tiles or shingles. Seller need not fix "Cosmetic Conditions" (aesthetic imperfections that don't affect Working Condition, e.g. pitted marcite, worn flooring, nail holes, minor cracks) unless caused by a defect Seller must fix. Cracked roof tiles, curling or worn shingles or limited roof life aren't defects without evidence of actual leaks or structural damage.
- (iii) **Within 10 days after receiving Buyer's notice or report**, Seller either completes the repairs, gets a licensed estimate and gives Buyer a copy, or gets a second Professional Inspector's report and estimates to Buyer. If the reports differ and the parties can't agree, they choose a third Professional Inspector together and split the cost equally; that report binds both.
- If repair cost is at or under the General Repair Limit, Seller makes the repairs per 12(f). If it's over, **within 5 days after a party's receipt of the last estimate**: (A) Seller may elect in writing to pay the excess, or (B) Buyer may designate which repairs Seller makes (up to the limit) and accept the rest "as is". **If neither party gives notice, either party may terminate**; Deposit refunded. The form sets no end date for that termination right; the safe reading is that it lasts until one party exercises it or the parties agree otherwise. Ask the agent.

**Standard 12(c) WDO inspection and repair** (lines 325–345):

- (i) A Florida-licensed pest control business may inspect. Buyer delivers the WDO report to Seller **within the Inspection Period** if any evidence of infestation or damage is found. WDO excludes fences.
- (ii) If Seller previously treated for the type found, Seller need not retreat if there's no visible live infestation, and Seller transfers a current full treatment warranty at Seller's cost. **Within 10 days after receiving the WDO report**, Seller gets licensed estimates of damage and treatment and delivers them. Seller makes treatments and repairs per 12(f) up to the WDO Repair Limit. If cost exceeds the limit, **within 5 days after receipt of Seller's estimate**, Buyer may agree to pay the excess, or designate repairs up to the limit and accept the rest "as is". **Seller has no election to pay the excess here** (unlike 12(b) and 12(d)). If Buyer gives no notice, either party may terminate; Deposit refunded.

**Standard 12(d) Permits** (lines 346–372):

- (i) Buyer may inspect records for open or expired permits and unpermitted improvements, and gives written notice **within the Inspection Period**. Seller promptly delivers plans and documents.
- (ii) **Within 10 days after receiving the notice**, Seller gets a licensed estimate to remedy and delivers it. **No later than 5 days before the Closing Date**, Seller, up to the Permit Limit, has open and expired permits (identified by Buyer or known to Seller) closed and required permits obtained and closed, and before the Closing Date provides written proof. If final inspections are delayed by the government, the **Closing Date extends up to 10 days**; failing that, either party may terminate, Deposit refunded.
- If cost exceeds the Permit Limit, **within 5 days after a party's receipt of the estimates**: (A) Seller may pay the excess, or (B) Buyer may accept the permit status "as is" and take **a credit at Closing equal to the Permit Limit**. If neither gives notice, either party may terminate; Deposit refunded.

**Standard 12(e) Walk-through** (lines 373–378): same timing as AS IS 12(b), and also verifies the required repairs and replacements were made.

**Standard 12(f) Repair standards; assignment** (lines 379–384): repairs are done in a good and workmanlike manner by an appropriately licensed person, to code, with materials equal or better than as of ED. Except for the WDO warranty in 12(c)(ii) (Seller's cost), assignable contracts and warranties are assigned at Buyer's option and cost.

### 13. Escrow Agent

**Both.** AS IS lines 293–312; Standard lines 386–405. Any Closing Agent or Escrow Agent holding the Deposit deposits it promptly, holds it in Florida and disburses it per the contract, subject to Collection. Uncollected funds don't excuse Buyer's performance. On conflicting demands or good faith doubt, Agent may hold the funds until the parties agree or a court decides, or deposit them with the circuit court clerk; a broker-agent follows Chapter 475, F.S. and FREC rules (mediation, arbitration, interpleader or escrow disbursement order). Agent recovers reasonable attorney's fees and costs from the escrowed funds when made a party or interpleading. Agent isn't liable for mis-delivery unless willful breach or gross negligence. Survives Closing or termination.

### 14. Professional Advice; Broker Liability

**Both.** AS IS lines 313–334; Standard lines 406–427. Broker advises verifying condition, square footage and facts with professionals. Buyer agrees to rely on Seller, inspectors and government agencies, not Broker. Each party indemnifies Broker for claims based on its own inaccurate information, misstatements or non-performance, tasks Broker did at its request outside Chapter 475, and vendors' products, services and expenses. Each party selects and pays its own vendors and other costs whether or not the deal closes. Broker is treated as a party for this paragraph. Survives Closing or termination.

### 15. Default

**Both.** AS IS lines 336–348; Standard lines 429–441.

- 15(a) Buyer default (including not paying the Deposit on time): Seller may keep the Deposit as agreed liquidated damages and full settlement, or seek specific performance (in equity) under Para. 16.
- 15(b) Seller default (other than failure to make title marketable after diligent effort): Buyer may get the Deposit back without waiving damages, and may sue for damages or specific performance under Para. 16.
- Survives Closing or termination.

### 16. Dispute Resolution

**Both.** AS IS lines 349–360; Standard lines 442–453. 16(a): the parties have **10 days after conflicting demands for the Deposit** to resolve the dispute, then must mediate. 16(b): mediation under Florida's mediator rules and Chapter 44, F.S.; the mediator must be certified or have real estate experience. Injunctive relief may be sought without mediating. Unsettled disputes go to court. Survives.

### 17. Attorney's Fees; Costs

**Both.** AS IS lines 361–365; Standard lines 454–458. Mediation fee split equally; each party pays its own mediation costs and attorney's fees. In litigation the prevailing party recovers costs and reasonable attorney's fees. Survives.

### 18. Standards

**Both, Different Text.** AS IS lines 366–590 (A through X); Standard lines 459–676 (A through V). See Standards A to X below.

### 19. Addenda

**Both, Different Text.** AS IS lines 592–593; Standard lines 678–679. Checked riders and addenda are incorporated. Both forms list A Condominium, B Homeowners' Assn., C Seller Financing, D Mortgage Assumption, E FHA/VA Financing, F Appraisal Contingency, G Short Sale, H Homeowners'/Flood Ins, J Interest-Bearing Acct., M Defective Drywall, N Coastal Construction Control Line, O Insulation Disclosure, P Lead Paint Disclosure (Pre-1978), Q Housing for Older Persons, R Rezoning, S Lease Purchase/Lease Option, T Pre-Closing Occupancy, U Post-Closing Occupancy, V Sale of Buyer's Property, W Back-up Contract, X Kick-out Clause, Y Seller's Attorney Approval, Z Buyer's Attorney Approval, AA Licensee Property Interest, BB Binding Arbitration, CC Miami-Dade County Special Taxing District Disclosure, DD Seasonal/Vacation Rentals, EE Qualifying Improvements Disclosure, FF Credit Related to Buyers Broker Compensation, GG Sellers Agreement with Respect to Buyers Broker Compensation, and Other.

- **AS IS: I, K and L are RESERVED.** A check next to I, K or L on an AS IS form is an error; ask what was meant.
- **Standard: I Mold Inspection, K "As Is", L Right to Inspect/Cancel.** K or L changes the Standard's inspection and repair rules (see Telling the Forms Apart).
- Rider terms are not in this file. A checked rider that isn't attached is a red flag.

### 20. Additional Terms

**Both.** AS IS lines 594–611; Standard lines 680–697. Free-text terms. Under Standard R, typed or handwritten terms control printed terms they conflict with, so read Para. 20 before relying on any default or printed rule (seller credits, repair caps, appraisal gaps, changed deadlines, leasebacks and so on).

### Counter-Offer Box

**Both.** AS IS lines 612–613; Standard lines 698–699. A checkbox: "Seller counters Buyer's offer." When checked, the document is a counter-offer; acceptance time is 2 days after delivery unless stated (Para. 3(a)), and the Effective Date is set by the last signature or initial and delivery (Para. 3(b)). The AS IS page 12 note says the contract continues "with Line 610 on Page 13", but page 13 starts at line 616: a typo in the form, not a missing page.

### Legal Notices and Foreign Buyer Notice

**Both.** AS IS lines 616–634; Standard lines 700–717. Legally binding contract, seek an attorney; form approval isn't an opinion on terms; an asterisk after a margin line number marks a blank (the asterisks may not print on every copy). Conveyances to foreign buyers: Part III of Chapter 692 (ss. 692.201–692.205, F.S.) restricts purchases by buyers associated with a "foreign country of concern"; **at time of purchase Buyer must provide a signed affidavit** complying with the Act.

### Signatures and Notice Addresses

**Both.** AS IS lines 635–642; Standard lines 718–725. Two Buyer and two Seller signature lines with dates, and each party's address for notice. Signature dates plus delivery fix the Effective Date (Para. 3(b)). Notices may also go to the party's broker or attorney (Standard O).

### Broker Block

**Both.** AS IS lines 643–652; Standard lines 726–735. The Listing and Cooperating Brokers named are the only brokers entitled to compensation. Seller and Buyer direct the Closing Agent to disburse the brokerage fees set by separate brokerage agreements and cooperative agreements, except fees the Broker already retained from escrow. The contract doesn't change any offer of compensation from Seller or Listing Broker to Cooperating Brokers. Names: Cooperating Sales Associate and Broker (if any), Listing Sales Associate and Broker. **The form sets no commission amount**; look for it in the brokerage agreements, Riders FF and GG, or Para. 20.

## Time Rules

- **Calendar days** (Standard F). Every period in these forms is in calendar days, based on where the Property is located. The forms use no business days. (Business-day periods come from outside the form: the condominium rider's statutory cancel period and the lender's 3-business-day Closing Disclosure rule.)
- **Time is of the essence** (Standard F). Late is late; there is no grace period.
- **Counting.** The form doesn't spell out whether the trigger day counts. The usual reading, which the skills use, is that it doesn't: day 1 is the day after the Effective Date or the triggering receipt. "At least X days before Closing Date" counts back from the Closing Date.
- **End of day.** The form sets no clock time. A period runs through its last day; notices "prior to expiration" of a period must be delivered by the end of the last day. The form doesn't say which hour ends the day; the safe practice is to deliver during business hours on or before the last day. Ask the agent if a notice was delivered late at night.
- **Weekend and holiday rollover** (Standard F). Any period or date in the contract, preprinted, handwritten, typed or inserted, that ends or falls on a Saturday, Sunday or national legal public holiday under 5 U.S.C. 6103(a), or a day such a holiday is observed because it fell on a weekend, extends to the next calendar day that is none of those. This includes the Closing Date and dates counted back from it. Read literally, a "before closing" date that lands on a weekend moves later, not earlier; to be safe, flag it and suggest acting by the prior business day.
- **Exception:** the rollover does not apply to the time for acceptance or the Effective Date (Para. 3). The counter-offer's 2 days don't roll over either.
- **Holidays** (5 U.S.C. 6103(a)): New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr.'s Birthday, Washington's Birthday, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day, plus the observed Friday or Monday when one falls on a weekend. Florida state holidays and Inauguration Day (not in 6103(a)) don't count.
- **Extensions that move dates:** Para. 5(a) CFPB (up to 7 days); Standard G Force Majeure (up to 7 days after the event ends; termination right past 30 days after the Closing Date); Standard A(ii) late Title Commitment (up to 5 days after receipt); Standard 12(d)(ii) permit final inspections (up to 10 days, Standard form only). Any other change needs a written amendment signed by the parties (Standard P).
- **Notices** (Standard O) must be written, by mail, fax, personal delivery or email, and count when given to or by the party's broker, licensee or attorney.

## Red Flags When Reading a Filled-In Contract

- [ ] **Form not identified** from title and footer, or a revision other than Rev. 2/26. Say so; never guess.
- [ ] **Numbers don't add up:** 2(a) + 2(b) + 2(c) + 2(d) + 2(e) differs from the Purchase Price, or a 2(c) percentage doesn't match the dollar figures.
- [ ] **Cash and financing both or neither checked** in Para. 8, or 8(a) checked while 2(c) shows a loan amount.
- [ ] **Effective Date unclear:** missing dates, signatures without delivery, or a counter-offer accepted after its 2-day window or after the 3(a) date.
- [ ] **Blanks with no default:** Closing Date, acceptance date, Escrow Agent, the 9(c) title box, 9(e) home warranty, loan type. Ask.
- [ ] **Blanks with a default:** use the default (see Blanks and Defaults) and tell the agent you did.
- [ ] **Handwritten or struck-through changes not initialed** by both parties. They control printed text only if agreed (Standards P and R).
- [ ] **Riders checked but not attached**, or attached but not checked.
- [ ] **RESERVED riders (I, K, L) checked on the AS IS form.**
- [ ] **Rider K ("As Is") or L (Right to Inspect/Cancel) on the Standard form:** the rider, not the Standard's printed Para. 12, controls. Get its text.
- [ ] **Para. 20 overrides printed terms** (seller credits, repair caps, appraisal gap, changed deadlines, leaseback, "as is" language on the Standard form). Read it before quoting any printed rule.
- [ ] **Repair limits on the Standard form** filled with both a dollar amount and a percentage that don't match, or only one of the three filled (the others default to 1.5%). Ask which controls if they conflict.
- [ ] **Deadlines that land on weekends or federal holidays**, including the Closing Date: apply the Standard F rollover.
- [ ] **Title Evidence Deadline** left blank on a cash deal (5 days) vs. financed (15 days): check which applies.
- [ ] **Para. 6(b) checked** (tenants or occupancy after Closing) without lease terms or estoppel plans; or possession before Closing without Rider T.
- [ ] **Inspection Period shorter than the loan or appraisal timeline** the parties expect, or longer than the Loan Approval Period, where it matters to the side you represent.
- [ ] **Assignability** box checked "may assign and be released" on a deal that isn't meant to be assignable.
- [ ] **Deposit to be delivered "with offer"** but no Escrow Agent named.
- [ ] **9(c)(iii) Miami-Dade/Broward option** checked for a property outside those counties.
- [ ] **Entity or trust buyer paying cash:** the FinCEN report (Standard I(iii)) may be required; information is due the day before Closing and Buyer pays the fees.
- [ ] **Foreign seller:** FIRPTA withholding up to 15% may exceed Seller's net proceeds (Standard V(iv)).
- [ ] **Pre-1978 home** without the lead paint rider (P); **HOA or condo** without Rider A or B; **flood zone A or V** without a plan for the 10(d) window.
- [ ] **Commission terms** expected in the contract: the broker block sets no amount; look for Riders FF or GG or Para. 20.
- [ ] **Two documents disagree** on a date or amount (contract vs. counter-offer vs. amendment): the latest executed document controls; flag the conflict.
